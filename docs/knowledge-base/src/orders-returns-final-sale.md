# Final Sale and Return Policy
## Which product categories are classified as final sale?

Clearance, custom-made, personalized, and perishable items are classified as final sale at Northvale. Customers can easily identify these restricted items because final-sale badges appear clearly at checkout and on the order confirmation. Northvale excludes these specific categories from standard return eligibility to maintain inventory integrity. Customers should review their cart carefully before completing any purchase involving these goods. No exceptions are granted for change-of-mind requests on items marked as final sale.

## Are promotional bundle items eligible for partial returns?

Promotional bundle items are governed by an all-or-nothing bundle return rule enforced by Northvale. Customers cannot return individual components of a promotional bundle separately under standard return guidelines. When bundled discounts are applied to a purchase, returning any part of the bundle requires the return of all associated items. Northvale calculates refunds based on the complete bundle unit rather than standalone item values. Customers seeking a refund on bundled merchandise must send back the entire promotional set together.

## What happens if I return only part of a multi-piece kit?

Returning only part of a multi-piece kit triggers a return deduction missing components rate of 25%. Northvale strictly requires customers to return all included accessories and manuals when submitting kit returns. If any components are absent from the package, the refund is automatically reduced by the specified deduction percentage. Customers must ensure all original parts accompany the shipment to avoid partial-refund penalties. Northvale inspects multi-piece returns thoroughly at the warehouse intake dock to verify completeness.

## Can opened consumables or hygiene-sealed products be returned?

Opened consumables and hygiene-sealed products cannot be returned once their protective seals are broken. Northvale enforces strict hygiene and safety seal restrictions to protect the health of all customers. Unopened items in this category remain eligible for return within standard guidelines if factory seals stay intact. Customers who open consumables or hygiene-sensitive goods forfeit their eligibility for a refund. Northvale rejects any opened items during the initial warehouse inspection process.

## What if a final-sale item arrives defective or damaged?

A defective-item override applies to final-sale exclusions when merchandise arrives damaged or non-functional. Northvale requires photographic evidence at intake review to process any defective final-sale claim. Customers must submit clear photos showing the damage before support authorizes the return process. Once verified, defective final-sale items qualify for appropriate remediation despite the initial restriction. Northvale ensures customers are not penalized for manufacturing defects or transit damage on final-sale purchases.

## How are gift purchases handled when returned by the recipient?

Gift purchases returned by the recipient result in store credit issuance rather than a cash refund. Northvale ensures the original purchaser is not notified of any gift return processed by the recipient. Gift recipients receive store credit equivalent to the item value upon successful intake inspection. Northvale processes these transactions discreetly to maintain privacy for both the buyer and the recipient. Customers initiating a gift return must provide the original order reference number for verification.

## What deductions apply to torn or defaced retail packaging?

Torn or defaced retail packaging incurs a return deduction damaged packaging rate of 15% on the refund amount. Northvale distinguishes shipping carton wear from damage sustained by the manufacturer retail box during transit or handling. The deduction is applied only when the product box itself is compromised rather than the outer mailer. Customers should pack items securely in protective outer shipping cartons to prevent box damage. Northvale inspects retail packaging condition during the standard warehouse evaluation phase.

## What happens when returned goods fail baseline intake inspection?

Returned goods that fail baseline intake inspection are subject to an adjusted refund rate failed inspection of 60%. Northvale determines inspection failure based on criteria such as heavy wear or missing parts upon arrival at the dock. Instead of receiving a full refund, customers receive the adjusted percentage payout for non-compliant items. Northvale applies this policy uniformly to discourage the return of heavily used or incomplete merchandise. Customers are notified of the inspection failure when the warehouse completes the evaluation.

## How are late returns outside the eligibility window treated?

Late returns outside the eligibility window are subject to discretionary acceptance and a mandatory restocking fee late return of 50%. Northvale imposes this substantial fee to offset the logistical costs of processing untimely merchandise. Customers whose returns are rejected have the option to request item return-to-sender at their own expense. Northvale evaluates late submissions on a case-by-case basis before granting any discretionary exceptions. Customers should always initiate returns well before the expiration of the designated window.

## How do free gift-with-purchase promotions affect a return?

Free gift-with-purchase promotions require customers to return the promotional gift alongside the qualifying item. Northvale applies a financial deduction if the promotional item is kept by the customer instead of being returned. The refund for the primary item is reduced by the retail value of the unreturned promotional gift. Northvale mandates the return of all promotional items to maintain the accuracy of the transaction balance. Customers must include every bundled promotional piece in the return package.

## Who covers return shipping on partial-kit or defective final-sale returns?

Return shipping fees for non-defective returns are set at a shipping return first item charge of $6.95 and a shipping return additional item charge of $2.50. Northvale confirms free return shipping for verified defective items or documented fulfillment errors. Customers returning partial kits or non-defective merchandise must cover these standard carrier fees. Northvale deducts the applicable shipping charges directly from the final refund total. Customers with verified defective final-sale items receive a prepaid return label from support.

## When is signature verification required on high-value bundle returns?

Signature verification is required at the intake dock whenever a return meets the return high_value signature threshold of $500. Northvale mandates this added security measure for expensive bundle returns to prevent transit loss and verify delivery. Customers are strongly advised by Northvale to keep their carrier drop-off receipt as proof of shipment. This receipt protects customers in the event of shipping disputes regarding high-value packages. Northvale tracks all high-value intake shipments with mandatory carrier signatures.

## How long does inspection take for final-sale exception claims?

Warehouse inspection for final-sale exception claims operates under a return inspection decision sla of 48 hours. Northvale staff review all exception claims within this timeframe once the package arrives at the facility. Customers can request an escalation for disputed condition grading if they disagree with the initial warehouse assessment. Northvale management reviews escalated claims to ensure fair application of all return rules. Customers receive an email notification detailing the final inspection decision.

## How are refunds calculated and settled for adjusted returns?

Refunds are calculated using itemized deduction calculations and settled according to a refund processing standard timeline of 5 to 7 business days. Northvale credits the funds back to the original method of payment or issues store credit in the case of gift returns. Customers can review the itemized breakdown on their return confirmation statement issued after inspection. Northvale ensures all approved deductions are clearly documented before the refund transfer occurs. The financial settlement typically appears in the customer account within the stated business day range.

## Can clearance items be exchanged instead of refunded?

Clearance items cannot be exchanged because final-sale restrictions also apply to change-of-mind exchanges at Northvale. Customers requesting a different size or color for a clearance product will have their request denied under standard policy. Northvale maintains a strict defect-only replacement exception for final-sale merchandise. Customers with defective clearance goods may receive an identical replacement if stock permits. Northvale does not process standard item swaps for clearance inventory.
