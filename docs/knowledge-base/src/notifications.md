# Notifications and Alerts FAQ

## What notification channels does Northvale use?

Northvale delivers account and order communications across several convenient digital channels designed to keep you informed. Email serves as our primary default channel for all transactional receipts, policy updates, and general account correspondence. You can also opt in to receive real-time SMS text messages for important account security verification and urgent carrier delivery updates. If you have installed our official mobile application on your smartphone, you can enable native mobile push notifications. Furthermore, alerts and updates are posted directly within the notifications tab of your online account portal. Finally, desktop users can opt in to receive web browser push notifications for timely updates while navigating our site.

## What notifications will I receive about my order?

When you complete a purchase, an order confirmation email containing your itemized receipt is generated and sent immediately at checkout. Once our warehouse team picks, packs, and dispatches your order, a dispatch notification including your active carrier tracking number is sent within 4 hours. Following delivery, a formal delivery confirmation email is dispatched immediately upon carrier drop-off scan. If a transit delay or exception occurs, delivery exception alerts are sent to you within 2 hours of the carrier flag being posted. Additionally, customers receive automated status updates at every stage of the return, refund, or exchange fulfillment lifecycle.

## What security alerts are sent to protect my account?

Our security monitoring system dispatches immediate automated security alerts whenever a login is detected originating from an unrecognized device hardware profile or new geographic location. You will receive an immediate notification whenever critical account credentials are changed, such as a password modification or adjustments to your two-factor authentication (2FA) setup. A security alert is also sent immediately if a change is made to the primary email address registered on your account. If the platform detects suspicious login behavior, defined as 3 or more failed login attempts within a 24-hour window, an alert is dispatched. Account lockout notices provide a direct self-service unlock link.

## How do I manage my marketing communication preferences?

You have full control over promotional communications and can choose to opt in to marketing emails during checkout or at any time via your account settings. Enrolling in promotional marketing SMS text messages requires a separate explicit opt-in distinct from transactional security and delivery SMS alerts. Similarly, receiving promotional push notifications on your mobile device is managed separately from critical order tracking alerts. Every single marketing email delivered by Northvale contains a direct, visible unsubscribe link in the footer. You can adjust your comprehensive notification settings, channels, and marketing subscriptions at any time through the notifications management page.

## How long does unsubscribing take and what are the rules?

When you submit an unsubscribe request for marketing communications, the modification takes operational effect within 24 hours of submission. Please note that transactional security alerts, authentication codes, and order status notifications cannot be unsubscribed from while maintaining an active account profile. Unsubscribing from all promotional marketing materials has no impact on the delivery of vital transactional emails, receipts, or account notices. If you receive promotional SMS messages, replying directly with "STOP" will immediately unsubscribe your mobile number from SMS marketing alerts. Northvale ensures all formal marketing unsubscribe requests are processed within 10 business days per CAN-SPAM regulations.
