# International Returns Policy
## Which countries are eligible for international returns?

International returns are eligible from all supported global destinations serviced by Northvale. Customers residing outside the United States may initiate returns according to established policy guidelines. Standard merchandise must be sent back within 30 days of the original delivery date. Electronic items must be returned within 15 days of the original delivery date. All cross-border shipments must comply with local export regulations.

## How do I obtain an international Return Merchandise Authorization?

An international Return Merchandise Authorization must be obtained through the official Northvale support portal before shipping any items back. Customers must submit an online request to receive the required return authorization documentation. Every international return requires a valid commercial invoice attached to the exterior of the parcel. Unauthorized shipments sent without an approved authorization document will be refused at the intake facility. Support agents can guide customers through the digital submission process upon request.

## Why are prepaid domestic labels unavailable for international returns?

Prepaid domestic labels are unavailable for international returns due to international carrier routing restrictions and cross-border customs regulations. Customers located outside the United States must arrange and pay for their own tracked courier services. Northvale does not generate or provide prepaid postage for cross-border return transit. Selecting a reliable international shipping provider ensures proper tracking visibility throughout the journey. Customers retain full responsibility for booking and paying for the return freight.

## What customs forms must accompany an international return parcel?

Every international return parcel must be accompanied by a commercial invoice, a valid Return Merchandise Authorization reference number, and the correct Harmonized System code fields. Missing customs documentation frequently causes severe border delays or outright refusal by customs authorities. Customers must securely attach these required forms to the outside of the return packaging in a transparent pouch. Failure to provide accurate classification details prevents successful customs clearance. Proper paperwork ensures the package moves smoothly back to the Northvale facility.

## How should returned goods be declared to avoid double import duty?

Returned goods should be declared specifically as Returned Goods in the original country to avoid incurring double import duties. Customers must instruct their chosen courier to use this precise customs description on all shipping paperwork. Misdeclaring the shipment as new merchandise triggers unnecessary import taxes and regulatory fees at the border. Northvale will not cover customs charges resulting from incorrect item descriptions or misdeclarations. Accurate declarations ensure that customs authorities recognize the shipment as a merchandise return.

## Are original international shipping fees and duties refunded?

Original international shipping fees and duties are generally non-refundable upon the completion of a return. The outbound international shipping base fee of $24.95 is retained by Northvale and is never returned. Customers seeking refunds for previously paid import duties must file a claim directly with their local customs authority. Northvale cannot reimburse tariffs or taxes collected by foreign governments during the initial import process. Refund disbursements are strictly limited to the purchase price of the returned merchandise.

## Which international carriers are accepted for return transit?

Accepted international carriers for return transit include major tracked global couriers that offer door-to-door delivery visibility. Customers must select a reputable shipping provider that supplies verifiable tracking information from origin to destination. Retaining the airway bill and tracking number is mandatory for verifying the shipment status. Untracked postal services are strictly prohibited for international returns due to high loss rates. Valid tracking documentation protects customers in the event of transit disputes or lost parcels.

## How long does international return transit and customs clearance take?

International return transit and customs clearance typically take 10 to 21 business days to reach the Northvale warehouse. Total delivery duration depends heavily on geographic location and international carrier performance. Customs hold times can vary significantly and remain entirely outside of Northvale control. Customers should monitor their tracking numbers closely during the cross-border journey. Delays at foreign export or import borders may extend the overall delivery timeframe.

## When is signature confirmation required on international returns?

Signature confirmation is required on international returns whenever the package contents exceed the high-value signature threshold of $500. This mandatory requirement ensures secure delivery receipt at the Northvale intake dock. Customers shipping expensive merchandise back across borders must select a signature-required delivery option through their courier. Additionally, purchasing shipping insurance is strongly recommended for all cross-border return shipments. Insuring the parcel protects the customer against loss or damage during international transit.

## How are international returns inspected after clearing customs?

International returns are inspected by warehouse staff immediately after clearing customs to determine eligibility for a refund. The warehouse inspection decision SLA is completed within 48 hours of the package arriving at the facility. Staff carefully examine each returned item for wear, alterations, and completeness. Photographic documentation is automatically captured for any transit-damaged returns arriving from abroad. This rigorous inspection process ensures accurate and fair refund processing for all international orders.

## What happens if an international return is rejected by customs?

International returns that are rejected by customs undergo standard return-to-sender handling back to the original shipper. Rejections typically occur due to unpaid duties, missing customs forms, or incorrect merchandise declarations. Customers remain entirely responsible for reshipment costs resulting from incomplete or inaccurate paperwork. Northvale cannot intervene in foreign customs disputes or pay outstanding border fees on behalf of customers. Ensuring all documents are correct before dispatch prevents customs rejections.

## In what currency are international return refunds issued?

International return refunds are issued strictly in the original transaction currency used during the initial purchase. Card issuers and financial institutions may apply a foreign exchange conversion fee ranging from 1% to 3% on the refunded amount. Northvale does not control or reimburse currency conversion fees charged by external banks. Exchange rate fluctuations between the purchase date and the refund date may cause minor discrepancies in the final credited amount. Customers should consult their financial institution regarding specific foreign transaction policies.

## How long do international card and PayPal refunds take?

International credit card refunds take a standard duration of 5 to 7 business days to process through financial networks. PayPal refunds are processed much faster within 48 hours of warehouse approval. Cross-border bank posting variances may cause additional delays before funds appear in the customer account. The exact posting time depends entirely on the receiving financial institution and country of origin. Customers will receive an automated email notification as soon as the refund is initiated by Northvale.

## How are defective international items handled for return costs?

Defective international items are eligible for support reimbursement of the verified return courier receipt. Customers must obtain formal pre-approval from support before dispatching any high-cost courier service for defective goods. Once the item is received and the defect is verified, shipping costs are credited back to the customer. Unauthorized shipping expenses exceeding standard rates will not be fully reimbursed by Northvale. Providing a valid shipping receipt is mandatory to process the freight cost reimbursement.

## Can multiple international orders be combined into one return parcel?

Multiple international orders cannot be combined into a single return parcel under any circumstances. Mixing Return Merchandise Authorizations across distinct commercial invoices violates international customs processing rules. Every return package requires a unique customs reconciliation tied specifically to one order number. Combining shipments causes severe processing delays and risks customs rejection at the border. Customers must ship each order in a separate package with its own distinct paperwork.
