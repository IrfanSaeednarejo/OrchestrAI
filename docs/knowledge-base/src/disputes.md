# Payment Dispute Policy

## When to File a Dispute

Customers may file a formal payment dispute to investigate unauthorized transactions, confirmed duplicate charges, or paid services that were never received. Conversely, customers must not file payment disputes for packages that remain actively in transit with a carrier or items currently within the standard return window. All payment dispute claims must be submitted within 60 days of the original transaction charge date to be eligible for review. If a dispute is filed regarding an approved refund that has not been received, the claim must include the official refund confirmation notice. Submitting a payment dispute immediately reverses and suspends any ongoing return or refund processing for that transaction.

## Internal Review Process

Northvale initiates an internal billing review within 2 business days of receiving an official dispute inquiry ticket. During this investigation, the specialized billing team conducts a comprehensive audit of the complete order history, payment records, and customer communications. The support team may reach out to the customer during this period to request additional supporting documentation if necessary. The billing department issues a formal internal dispute decision within 10 business days of ticket receipt. When a dispute is approved in the customer's favor, the corresponding refund is executed within 5 business days of the rendered decision.

## Chargeback Handling

When a customer initiates a formal bank chargeback, the notification automatically triggers an internal dispute investigation case within Northvale's payment operations. Northvale compiles and submits evidence to the merchant bank within the card issuer's mandated response deadline, which typically ranges from 20 to 45 days. While an active chargeback is being reviewed by the bank, the customer account is flagged and prevented from placing any new orders. If a chargeback dispute is ultimately resolved in Northvale's favor, the system re-bills the original payment method for the disputed amount. If resolved in the customer's favor, the internal case is closed.

## Documentation Requirements

Customers submitting a formal payment dispute must furnish their order number and the exact payment method used for the purchase. The submission must include an official bank or credit card statement clearly demonstrating the disputed charge line item. In addition, the customer must attach copies of any prior relevant correspondence conducted with Northvale customer support. For claims asserting unauthorized fraudulent charges, filing a formal police report is strictly mandatory for any dispute exceeding $500. When filing disputes for orders or services allegedly not received, customers must supply official carrier tracking data and photographic evidence.

## Timeline and Resolution

The billing department sends a formal dispute receipt acknowledgment within 24 hours of ticket submission. The full internal investigation is completed and a resolution determined within 10 business days. For disputes escalated through financial institutions, formal bank chargeback timelines are established by the card issuer and typically span 20 to 45 days. When an internal dispute is resolved in favor of the customer, any re-issued refund appears on the funding account within 5 business days of resolution. Any closed dispute that a customer seeks to re-open after 30 days have elapsed requires submitting an entirely fresh dispute filing.
