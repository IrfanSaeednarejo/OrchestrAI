# Account Privacy Retention Policy
## What is Northvale’s data retention philosophy?

Northvale enforces strict data minimization principles and purpose-limited retention schedules to protect your personal information. Data is kept only as long as necessary to fulfill specific operational and legal obligations. Once these statutory windows expire, records are permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized. This approach minimizes unnecessary exposure and upholds high privacy standards across all customer interactions. Routine audits ensure that all internal systems strictly adhere to these established retention thresholds.

## How long are transactional order and billing records retained?

Transactional order and billing records are securely retained for 7 years following the date of purchase. This duration complies directly with federal tax regulations and statutory accounting requirements governing commercial transactions. Preserving these ledgers ensures accurate financial reporting and validates past purchases for authorized account holders. Order histories remain accessible in the customer portal throughout this mandatory period. After 7 years elapse, these financial records undergo secure destruction protocols.

## How long are security logs and fraud-prevention records kept?

Security logs and fraud-prevention records are kept for 5 years to safeguard the platform against unauthorized access. This retention period supports thorough chargeback defense and facilitates repeat-abuse prevention across the infrastructure. Access logs, IP telemetry, and threat detection reports aid security teams in investigating suspicious activities. Retaining these records for 5 years ensures long-term system integrity and reliable audit trails. Once the 5-year period expires, security telemetry is securely purged from active servers.

## How long are customer support chat transcripts and tickets retained?

Customer support chat transcripts and support tickets are retained for 3 years from the date of closure. This timeframe supports comprehensive quality assurance programs and aids in the resolution of recurring customer inquiries. Support agents reference previous interactions to maintain continuity and provide accurate assistance for complex issues. Keeping these records for 3 years balances customer service needs with data minimization goals. Following the 3-year retention window, support logs are automatically scrubbed from active customer support databases.

## How long is digital marketing and advertising telemetry stored?

Digital marketing and advertising telemetry is stored for 2 years before being permanently removed from analytics systems. This duration distinguishes marketing log files from core transactional ledgers and operational databases. Storing campaign interaction data for 2 years allows marketing analysts to measure long-term campaign effectiveness. Telemetry data helps optimize promotional strategies without retaining personal identifiers indefinitely. When the 2-year lifecycle concludes, all associated advertising telemetry undergoes secure deletion.

## How long do deleted records persist in encrypted system backups?

Deleted records persist in encrypted system backups for a maximum purge cycle of 30 days before complete removal. Northvale utilizes immutable backup rotation schedules rather than performing selective edits on archival tapes. This 30-day backup persistence window ensures system resilience and disaster recovery capabilities without violating core deletion requests. Backups naturally overwrite themselves as the rotation cycle progresses past the 30-day threshold. Consequently, data removed from active databases is completely cleared from archives within this timeframe.

## How are payment card details tokenized and retained?

Payment card details are tokenized and securely retained in compliance with strict payment card industry security standards. Full credit card numbers are handled exclusively by certified third-party payment processors rather than touched by Northvale servers. Northvale retains only the last-4 digits, card brand, and expiration date for user interface display purposes. This tokenization method ensures that sensitive financial credentials are never exposed during transactions. Customers can manage saved payment methods safely through these protected token vaults.

## Which categories of third-party sub-processors receive customer data?

Third-party sub-processors that receive customer data are strictly limited to payment processors, shipping carriers, cloud hosting providers, and email delivery services. Each category of sub-processor fulfills a vital operational function necessary for order fulfillment and account management. Northvale binds all external vendors to strict contractual Data Processing Agreements that enforce rigorous security controls. These agreements prohibit sub-processors from using transferred data for any unauthorized secondary purposes. Vendor compliance is continuously monitored by internal privacy and legal oversight teams.

## Does Northvale share customer addresses with shipping carriers?

Northvale shares customer addresses with shipping carriers exclusively for fulfillment necessity. Essential details such as the recipient name, street address, and contact phone or email alerts are provided to ensure accurate delivery. Shipping partners are strictly prohibited from using these customer addresses for secondary marketing or promotional campaigns. Data transmitted to logistics providers is encrypted both in transit and at rest. Carriers must delete shipping data in accordance with their own statutory obligations and contractual agreements.

## How is data protected during cross-border transfers to sub-processors?

Data is protected during cross-border transfers to sub-processors through the implementation of Standard Contractual Clauses and robust encryption. All personal information is encrypted both in transit across public networks and at rest within secure cloud environments. Regional hosting safeguards ensure that data remains localized whenever regulatory frameworks demand specific geographical boundaries. Independent security audits verify that all cross-border transmission channels meet rigorous corporate standards. These multi-layered safeguards maintain confidentiality and data integrity across international operations.

## What happens to retention schedules when a legal hold or dispute is active?

Retention schedules are immediately suspended when a legal hold or customer dispute is officially active. Routine data purges are halted for any records relevant to ongoing litigation, regulatory inquiries, or financial chargebacks. Suspending standard deletion timelines ensures the preservation of critical evidence and maintains legal compliance. Once the dispute or litigation fully concludes, the normal retention schedule resumes for the affected records. This controlled exception process guarantees that evidence integrity is never compromised by automated deletion routines.

## How are analytics datasets anonymized after retention windows expire?

Analytics datasets are anonymized after retention windows expire by stripping all direct personal identifiers from the underlying databases. Once direct identifiers are removed, remaining metrics are aggregated to study macro-level sales trends and platform performance. This anonymization process ensures that the resulting analytical datasets are completely non-reidentifiable. Anonymized data may be kept indefinitely for business intelligence and long-term capacity planning. Customers cannot be singled out or identified within these aggregated statistical reports.

## Are multi-agent AI support traces included in support record retention?

Multi-agent AI support traces are fully included in the standard support record retention schedule. All automated chat logs and AI interaction transcripts follow the designated retention lifecycle applied to human support tickets. Advanced automated tools ensure that any sensitive personally identifiable information within AI traces undergoes immediate redaction. These operational logs help improve automated response accuracy and service quality over time. Following the expiration of the retention window, all AI support traces are permanently deleted.

## How often does Northvale audit sub-processor compliance?

Northvale conducts annual security reviews and SOC2 compliance assessments for all active sub-processors and privacy vendors. These periodic audits evaluate vendor technical controls, data handling practices, and incident response readiness. Contracts empower Northvale with the explicit right to terminate relationships with any vendor failing to meet required standards. Audit reports are reviewed by executive leadership and security committees to ensure continuous risk mitigation. This proactive oversight maintains a secure vendor ecosystem for all customer accounts.

## Where can customers request a summary of active sub-processors?

Customers can request a summary of active sub-processors by reviewing the comprehensive schedule published within the main Privacy Policy. Inquiries regarding specific vendor relationships can also be directed to the designated privacy contact email address. Northvale commits to providing advance notice to registered users prior to implementing any material changes to the sub-processor list. This transparent communication ensures that customers remain fully informed about third-party data handlers. Dedicated support representatives are available to answer clarifying questions regarding vendor data access.
