# International Shipping Policy and FAQ
## Which countries does Northvale ship to internationally?

Northvale ships to supported international regions using a global carrier network while excluding restricted destination countries due to local regulations. The international shipping program covers major global territories outside domestic borders. Customers must select their specific country at checkout to confirm route eligibility. Certain remote or embargoed regions remain blocked from export. Carrier handoffs occur seamlessly through established international logistics partners.

## How much does international shipping cost at checkout?

Northvale assesses a standard international base shipping fee of $24.95 at checkout with weight-based scaling applied to heavier parcels. The international shipping rate accounts for cross-border transit expenses and customs documentation handling. Customers should note that the Alaska and Hawaii domestic surcharge of $12.00 is separate from international shipping charges. Total shipping costs appear clearly in the final order summary before payment submission. Additional carrier fees may apply depending on final destination rules.

## How long does international delivery typically take?

Northvale international delivery typically takes 10 to 21 business days to arrive at the destination address. This delivery window includes standard cross-border transit times managed by international courier networks. The warehouse pick-and-pack preparation SLA requires 24 hours before orders leave the fulfillment center. Customers can track their parcels continuously throughout the international journey. Remote delivery locations may experience slight variations within this established transit timeframe.

## What is the difference between DDP and DDU customs clearance?

Delivered Duty Paid and Delivered Duty Unpaid represent two distinct customs clearance methods applied at checkout. Delivered Duty Paid means all applicable import taxes and duties are collected during the initial purchase transaction. Delivered Duty Unpaid leaves import taxes and duties uncollected at checkout for the recipient to settle upon arrival. Customers must review their selected clearance model to understand impending carrier demands. Local customs authorities dictate which specific clearance protocols apply to incoming shipments.

## Are import duties and VAT included in my order total?

Import duties and value-added tax are included in the order total only when Delivered Duty Paid is selected at checkout. If the order processes under Delivered Duty Unpaid terms, those fees remain uncollected and require payment upon carrier delivery. Local customs authority assessment rules determine the exact percentage of tax applied to cross-border merchandise. Customers are responsible for verifying local tax thresholds prior to final purchase confirmation. The order invoice explicitly itemizes any pre-collected government levies.

## When will my international tracking number become active?

Northvale ensures international tracking numbers become active within the shipping tracking activation SLA of 4 hours after warehouse dispatch. The system generates a dispatch notification email matching the notification dispatch delay of 4 hours following carrier pickup. Customers can click the tracking link to view initial origin scan details once activated. International carriers may require additional time to populate intermediate transit checkpoints. Support teams monitor these activation timelines closely to ensure operational transparency.

## Why does international tracking pause during customs clearance?

International tracking frequently pauses during customs clearance due to handoff scan gaps between origin and destination carriers. These temporary visibility drops occur while government officials inspect parcel documentation at the border. The notification transit exception delay of 2 hours applies if structural transit alerts trigger during the review. Customers should expect standard processing pauses while packages move through secure border facilities. Normal tracking updates resume immediately once the local postal network assumes custody.

## When is a physical signature required for international delivery?

A physical signature is required for international delivery whenever the order value meets or exceeds the international signature threshold of $200. This mandatory security measure protects high-value cross-border merchandise from porch theft and delivery disputes. If the recipient is unavailable, the carrier coordinates redelivery or holds the parcel at a local pickup depot. Customers receive delivery attempt notices containing depot location details and retrieval instructions. Unclaimed parcels face standard carrier holding limits before initiating return procedures.

## What happens if I refuse to pay customs duties on delivery?

Refusing to pay customs duties on delivery results in the carrier initiating a return-to-sender or parcel abandonment process. Northvale deducts any applicable return transit and customs fees from the final customer refund amount. Undeliverable international parcels follow strict cross-border logistics guidelines established by the handling courier. Customers forfeit original outbound shipping fees when parcels are returned due to unpaid import taxes. Support representatives cannot reverse mandatory customs charges levied by sovereign import authorities.

## Can I change the destination country after placing an international order?

Northvale strictly prohibits cross-country address rerouting once an international order has completed checkout processing. Customers who need to modify shipping destinations must cancel the original transaction before dispatch if eligible. The warehouse pick-and-pack preparation SLA of 24 hours limits the available cancellation window. Placing a new order with the correct international address remains necessary after a successful cancellation. Post-dispatch address changes violate cross-border carrier routing agreements.

## How are split shipments handled on international orders?

Northvale handles split shipments on international orders through consolidated customs dispatch where feasible or synchronized multi-parcel tracking. Customers confirm a single international base shipping charge per order regardless of how many individual boxes depart the warehouse. Each box receives a distinct tracking number linked to the primary master order reference. Customs authorities review consolidated shipments according to local import manifest regulations. Splitting packages never incurs duplicate base shipping fees for the customer.

## What happens if local customs requests additional recipient ID?

Local customs requests for additional recipient ID require customers to provide tax identification numbers or national identification verification directly to the courier. Couriers reach out via email or phone when local import regulations mandate extra documentation for clearance. Failure to answer these verification requests promptly leads to severe clearance delays and potential parcel return. Customers should monitor their contact channels closely during the international delivery window. Northvale cannot override sovereign customs identification mandates.

## Are restricted or hazardous items blocked from international air freight?

Restricted and hazardous items are strictly blocked from international air freight due to global aviation safety export limits. Lithium ion batteries, pressurized aerosols, and other regulated goods fail export compliance standards for cross-border flights. Checkout address validation blocks these prohibited products automatically when an international destination is selected. Customers attempting to purchase restricted goods for overseas delivery must remove them from the cart. Compliance teams regularly update system filters to reflect changing international transport bans.

## How do local public holidays affect international delivery estimates?

Local public holidays affect international delivery estimates because standard business-day counting strictly excludes both origin and destination holidays. International transit windows account for foreign bank holidays and regional non-working days across global networks. Peak-season customs backlogs introduce additional operational buffers that extend transit timeframes. Customers should factor local holiday closures into their expected delivery calculations. Courier operations pause during officially recognized public celebrations in transit countries.

## What if an international shipment is returned as undeliverable?

An international shipment returned as undeliverable undergoes standard warehouse intake processing upon arrival back at the fulfillment center. Northvale inspects the returned merchandise and issues a refund minus the original outbound shipping charges. Customers seeking a replacement must place a completely new order through the standard checkout portal. Undeliverable parcels resulting from incorrect addresses or unfulfilled customs duties follow this exact recovery procedure. Return processing times depend on international courier transit durations back to the facility.
