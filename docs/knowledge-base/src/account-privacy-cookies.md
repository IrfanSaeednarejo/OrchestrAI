# Northvale Account Privacy and Cookies Policy
## What types of cookies and local storage does Northvale use?

Northvale uses strictly necessary, functional, analytics, and advertising cookies alongside local storage mechanisms to operate the platform. These technologies encompass both session tokens that expire when the browser closes and persistent tokens that remain stored until their set expiration date. Strictly necessary cookies enable core website functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. Functional cookies remember user choices to provide enhanced and personalized features during visits. Analytics and advertising cookies collect information about browsing habits to help tailor marketing campaigns and measure site performance.

## Which cookies are strictly necessary and cannot be disabled?

Strictly necessary cookies encompass authentication session identifiers, shopping cart contents, Cross-Site Request Forgery protection tokens, and fraud-prevention telemetry mechanisms. These specific cookies cannot be disabled through the standard preference center because they are vital for the fundamental operation of the website. Blocking strictly necessary cookies within browser settings will directly cause site breakage and prevent users from securely logging in or completing purchases. Northvale relies on these foundational technologies to maintain platform integrity, session continuity, and transaction security. Users attempting to disable these required elements will encounter significant operational failures across the digital storefront.

## How does Northvale honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals?

Northvale automatically detects browser-level Global Privacy Control headers and signals transmitted by visiting user agents. The platform treats any detected Global Privacy Control signal as a valid, binding opt-out request regarding ad-tech sharing and targeted advertising sales. This automated detection eliminates the need for manual preference adjustments when utilizing compliant browsers or browser extensions. The system respects the signal across all compatible pages to restrict unauthorized data telemetry collection immediately. Customers employing this preference standard receive continuous protection without requiring separate opt-out submissions for each session.

## How can I manage or withdraw my cookie consent preferences?

Customers can manage or withdraw their cookie consent preferences at any time by accessing the Cookie Preferences center located in the website footer. This dedicated management tool allows users to toggle specific non-essential cookie categories on or off according to their individual privacy requirements. Changes made within the preference center take effect immediately for all future page loads and subsequent browsing actions. Northvale records these consent choices locally to ensure the browser strictly adheres to the selected telemetry parameters. Users may revisit this interface whenever necessary to update their tracking configurations.

## How long are digital marketing telemetry records retained?

Digital marketing telemetry records are retained for a maximum duration of 2 years before undergoing systematic deletion or permanent anonymization. Northvale limits this retention window to ensure customer data is not kept longer than necessary for digital marketing analysis. Once the 2 years have elapsed, individual telemetry logs are either permanently erased or aggregated into anonymous statistical reports. This aggregation process strips away all identifiable markers to protect user privacy while preserving macro-level site performance insights. The established retention schedule applies uniformly to all standard marketing and advertising telemetry datasets collected across the platform.

## How do I opt out of behavioral advertising and retargeting pixels?

Users can opt out of behavioral advertising and retargeting pixels by disabling advertising cookies directly within the website preference center. Northvale also fully supports industry opt-out tools and recognizes Global Privacy Control signals as valid opt-out mechanisms for ad-tech tracking. Disabling these advertising features prevents third-party partners from deploying retargeting pixels and collecting cross-site browsing telemetry. Customers utilizing these opt-out pathways ensure their browsing activities remain unlinked from behavioral advertising profiles. The applied restrictions remain active until the user manually updates their browser cookies or preference settings.

## How long does a marketing communication opt-out take to apply?

A marketing communication opt-out takes effect within a operational window of 24 hours across primary distribution databases, complying with the CAN-SPAM maximum SLA of 10 business days. Northvale processes all unsubscribe requests promptly to ensure promotional messages cease within the regulatory timeframe. This operational opt-out effect window applies specifically to email newsletters and direct promotional notifications managed by the marketing department. Customers must note that this communication unsubscribe process remains distinct from browser-level cookie preference modifications. Separate actions are required if a user wishes to clear tracking cookies in addition to stopping direct marketing emails.

## Does Northvale use session-replay or error-telemetry tools?

Northvale utilizes anonymized error diagnostics tools that incorporate strict masking protocols for personally identifiable information and payment details. These diagnostic utilities capture system error codes and performance metrics to help engineers identify and resolve technical issues quickly. Password fields, credit card numbers, and other sensitive personal data inputs are permanently excluded from telemetry captures. The system actively screens all captured data streams to prevent the inadvertent storage of confidential user credentials. This controlled error-telemetry approach ensures platform reliability is maintained without compromising individual data privacy.

## What happens to analytics tracking when I use incognito or private browsing?

Incognito or private browsing modes create ephemeral session states that are completely cleared when the user closes the browser window. Analytics tracking tools operate within these temporary storage limits, meaning persistent identifiers cannot be easily retained across separate browsing sessions. However, if a user logs into a personal account during a private browsing session, account-level privacy preferences will still apply to that specific session. Local tracking cookies deployed during an incognito session expire automatically upon termination of the browser process. This temporary tracking environment offers enhanced isolation but relies on browser-level enforcement mechanisms.

## Are third-party social media pixels loaded without consent?

Third-party social media pixels are strictly gated and will not load without explicit user consent in legally regulated jurisdictions. Northvale blocks external ad tags and social media tracking scripts until the visitor actively opts into advertising cookies via the preference center. Furthermore, the detection of a Global Privacy Control signal automatically suppresses the loading of these third-party marketing elements. This consent-gated loading architecture ensures external entities cannot harvest visitor telemetry without prior authorization. Users who decline non-essential cookies experience a completely sanitized page load free from unauthorized social media trackers.

## How does Northvale separate essential security telemetry from ad tracking?

Northvale maintains a strict architectural separation between essential security telemetry and commercial advertising cookies. Fraud detection signals, rate-limit monitors, and system defense logs operate independently from marketing data collection pipelines. Essential security checks and authentication verifications remain fully active even when advertising cookies are completely disabled by the user. This separation ensures that protecting the platform against malicious activity does not rely on tracking user behavior for commercial gain. Visitors retain the right to reject marketing telemetry while maintaining the robust security protections required for safe browsing.

## Can mobile app or mobile web users configure the same privacy controls?

Mobile app and mobile web users can configure the same comprehensive privacy controls through a unified Cookie Preferences interface accessible across all responsive web platforms. Northvale ensures that privacy settings established on desktop environments align seamlessly with mobile browser experiences. Additionally, mobile applications respect operating system-level settings such as the Limit Ad Tracking preference provided by mobile device vendors. This multi-platform consistency allows customers to maintain uniform privacy parameters regardless of the device used to access the store. Users can manage their tracking preferences directly within the mobile application settings menu or browser footer.

## Do cookie preferences sync across my logged-in devices?

Cookie preferences stored locally in browser storage do not automatically sync across separate logged-in devices because consent states are tied to individual browsers rather than user accounts. Account-level marketing preferences stored in the user profile manage email communications, but cookie consent relies on browser-specific local parameters. Customers utilizing multiple devices or browsers must configure their tracking preferences independently on each platform. The presence of a Global Privacy Control signal on a specific browser will govern that environment regardless of account login status. Users should verify their cookie selections on every device used to access Northvale to ensure consistent telemetry management.

## Where can I view a list of active cookie categories and durations?

Customers can view a comprehensive list of active cookie categories and their exact storage durations by accessing the Cookie Preference modal details. This informational panel outlines every cookie utilized by Northvale, categorized by strictly necessary, functional, analytics, and advertising classifications. The platform performs periodic cookie-inventory updates to ensure the displayed retention periods and category descriptions remain accurate and transparent. Users reviewing this modal receive precise data regarding the lifespan of each persistent and session token deployed on the site. This inventory serves as the definitive reference for all telemetry tools operating within the digital ecosystem.

## Who can I contact with questions about web tracking or telemetry?

Customers with specific questions regarding web tracking practices or telemetry collection can contact the privacy compliance team through the designated support portal. The privacy compliance group manages all inquiries related to cookie operations, Global Privacy Control implementation, and data governance policies. Northvale ensures a prompt review of all tracking reports and compliance inquiries submitted by site visitors. Dedicated specialists investigate each submission to provide accurate clarifications concerning data collection methodologies and privacy rights. Users may initiate contact whenever they require further assistance regarding their digital footprint on the platform.
