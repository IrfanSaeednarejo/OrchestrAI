# Refund Policy

## Refund Eligibility

Monetary refunds are available exclusively for eligible items returned within the established 30-day return window. Refunds are issued strictly after the returned merchandise has been physically received and passed inspection at the intake facility. Original order shipping charges remain entirely non-refundable in standard circumstances unless the returned item was confirmed defective or shipped in error. Once approved, refunds are processed for the full original item price minus any applicable policy deductions. Certain merchandise categories, including custom-made goods, perishable items, and products designated as final sale, are completely non-refundable.

## Refund Timing

Every returned shipment undergoes physical inspection and receives a formal refund approval decision within 48 hours of warehouse intake. Once approved, the funds are credited back to the original funding source within 5 to 7 business days following approval. For transactions originally processed on debit cards, the credited funds may require up to 10 business days to appear on the bank statement. Electronic bank transfers executed via automated clearing house (ACH) typically settle within 3 business days after approval. When customer compensation or return resolution is issued as store credit, the credit balance is made available immediately upon approval with no holding delay.

## Partial Refunds

Returned merchandise received with missing components or unreturned accessories incurs a deduction of up to 25% from the final refund amount. Products returned with damaged, torn, or defaced manufacturer packaging incur a standard 15% packaging deduction provided the product itself remains fully intact. Late returns received outside the standard 30-day window are accepted only at Northvale's discretion and are subject to a mandatory 50% restocking fee. Merchandise returned in a condition that fails to meet baseline inspection requirements may be refunded at an adjusted rate of 60%. All partial refund calculations and itemized deductions are transparently detailed in the email confirmation.

## Refund Methods

All monetary refunds are issued strictly to the original payment method utilized during the initial checkout transaction, without exception. Purchases made using a gift card are refunded exclusively as store credit credited back to the customer's gift card balance. Transactions completed using split payment methods have their refund amounts allocated proportionally across each respective payment method originally charged. Approved refunds for purchases completed via PayPal are returned directly to the originating PayPal account within 48 hours. For orders placed through Klarna, issuing a refund automatically cancels remaining unpaid installments while previously paid installments are refunded.

## Shipping Fee Refunds

Original outbound shipping fees are refunded only when the customer returns merchandise that was confirmed defective or erroneously shipped by Northvale. Paid upgrades for expedited shipping services remain strictly non-refundable in all other standard return circumstances. For standard change-of-mind customer returns, the mandatory return shipping postage fee is deducted directly from the final issued refund balance. Optional fees paid for package protection insurance during checkout are entirely non-refundable once an order has dispatched. However, all return shipping postage costs are fully refunded to the customer whenever the underlying return was necessitated by Northvale's operational error.

## Refund Disputes

Customers wishing to challenge a refund determination must raise a formal dispute within 60 days of the original refund issue date. All submitted dispute cases are thoroughly investigated and reviewed within 5 business days by the specialized Northvale billing team. Initiating a formal bank chargeback supersedes any ongoing internal review process, as governed by our overarching payment disputes policy. Customers submitting a dispute must provide required documentation, including the order number, refund confirmation notice, and a formal bank statement showing no credit received. Following review, approved dispute resolutions trigger a manual refund re-issue within 24 hours.
