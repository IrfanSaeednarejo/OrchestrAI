# Northvale Shipping, PO Box, and Freight Policy
## Which shipping tiers can deliver to a US PO Box?

Standard shipping can deliver packages to a US PO Box through USPS compatibility, while private couriers enforce strict street address limits. Standard shipping utilizes a transit window of 5 to 7 business days for these deliveries. Expedited shipping and next-day shipping generally require physical street addresses instead of postal boxes. Northvale automatically filters incompatible shipping methods during the checkout process when a postal box is entered. Customers must supply a valid postal box format to ensure proper delivery routing by the postal service.

## What are the fees and free-shipping thresholds for domestic tiers?

Standard shipping is free when orders meet the minimum threshold of $50, whereas expedited shipping incurs a fee of $9.95, and next-day shipping incurs a fee of $19.95. These domestic shipping fees and free-shipping eligibility thresholds are calculated based on the order subtotal before applicable taxes and promotional discounts are applied. Standard orders arrive within 5 to 7 business days, expedited orders arrive within 2 to 3 business days, and next-day orders arrive in 1 business day. Customers can view exact shipping costs during the final checkout review stage before submitting payment.

## How are APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses shipped?

APO, FPO, and DPO military addresses are shipped exclusively via official military postal routing handled through USPS. Orders bound for military installations undergo standard warehouse processing within the warehouse pick-pack SLA of 24 hours. Actual transit duration for military addresses varies due to specialized defense postal logistics and security handoffs. Northvale applies standard domestic shipping rules to the initial domestic leg of military shipments. Customers should format military address fields correctly to prevent routing delays at postal consolidation centers.

## Why do Alaska and Hawaii orders incur an additional shipping fee?

Alaska and Hawaii orders incur a mandatory shipping fee of $12.00 across all available shipping tiers due to geographic distance. This surcharge covers specialized air and barge carrier transport costs required for non-contiguous state deliveries. Standard shipments to these regions maintain the usual transit window of 5 to 7 business days despite remote routing. Northvale applies this flat surcharge transparently at checkout whenever a non-contiguous postal code is entered. Customers are advised to review all destination-based surcharges prior to finalizing order placement.

## Can expedited or next-day shipping deliver to PO Boxes or military bases?

Expedited and next-day shipping cannot deliver to PO Boxes or military bases because air-courier networks strictly require physical street addresses. Next-day shipping operates on a transit schedule of 1 business day, which relies exclusively on direct-to-door courier infrastructure. Expedited shipping follows a window of 2 to 3 business days and similarly excludes postal boxes and military routing numbers. The checkout system enforces an automatic tier restriction that disables air delivery options for invalid address types. Customers attempting to select fast delivery for a postal box must provide a valid physical street address.

## How are oversized or heavy freight items scheduled for delivery?

Oversized or heavy freight items are scheduled for delivery through direct freight carrier appointment scheduling conducted by phone or SMS. Freight carriers coordinate delivery windows directly with recipients before final transport to the destination. Customers may choose between curbside delivery and threshold delivery options depending on the specific item classification. The warehouse processes freight orders within the standard warehouse pick-pack SLA of 24 hours before handing off to the freight carrier. Recipients must ensure a reachable phone number is attached to the order for timely appointment coordination.

## Can oversized freight items be delivered to a PO Box?

Oversized freight items cannot be delivered to a PO Box due to strict postal facility and locker dimension limits. Freight shipments require a physical street address and a reachable phone number to facilitate carrier appointment scheduling. Standard parcel services operate within a transit window of 5 to 7 business days, whereas freight requires specialized handling. Northvale mandates valid street addresses for all heavy cargo to prevent immediate delivery refusal by carriers. Customers attempting freight delivery to a postal box will be prompted to update their shipping destination.

## When is an adult signature required at residential delivery?

An adult signature is required at residential delivery for high-value purchases and heavy freight shipments to ensure secure handoff. If a recipient is unavailable during the initial visit, the carrier initiates the established carrier redelivery attempt policy. Standard parcel tiers such as expedited shipping taking 2 to 3 business days or next-day shipping taking 1 business day follow specific signature protocols. Freight carriers specifically mandate an authorized signature on the delivery receipt before unloading cargo. Customers receive tracking notifications detailing whether a direct signature is mandatory for their specific parcel.

## What happens after repeated failed delivery attempts at a street address?

After repeated failed delivery attempts at a street address, the carrier holds the package for a duration of 7 days before initiating a return to sender. During this carrier hold window, recipients can arrange for depot pickup by presenting a valid government-issued photo identification. Standard shipments with a transit duration of 5 to 7 business days are subject to these same carrier holding rules. Unclaimed packages returned to the facility are processed according to standard inventory intake procedures. Customers are encouraged to monitor tracking updates to retrieve packages before the holding period expires.

## How are rural or remote-area delivery surcharges handled?

Rural or remote-area delivery surcharges are handled through extended-area carrier routing that may add a minor transit buffer to normal delivery times. Standard shipments normally take 5 to 7 business days, but remote locations might experience slight extensions based on local courier schedules. Northvale ensures transparent surcharge display at checkout so customers see any remote delivery adjustments prior to payment. These destination fees reflect additional carrier handoffs required to reach isolated geographical regions. Customers ordering to remote addresses will find all applicable routing details summarized in their order confirmation.

## Can I request delivery to a parcel locker or carrier access point?

Delivery to a parcel locker or carrier access point can be requested using carrier hold-at-location redirection options after dispatch. Bulky items are subject to strict locker dimension limits and may be ineligible for secure locker storage. Standard orders taking 5 to 7 business days can often be redirected through the carrier portal once tracking activates. Northvale recommends verifying dimensional limits with the local access point before requesting package redirection. Customers utilizing access points must retrieve their items within the local holding limits established by the carrier.

## How does warehouse processing affect next-day delivery cutoffs?

Warehouse processing affects next-day delivery cutoffs by adhering strictly to the warehouse pick-pack SLA of 24 hours for order preparation. Orders placed after the daily operational cutoff time begin processing on the following business day. Next-day shipping provides a transit window of 1 business day once the carrier collects the dispatched parcel from the facility. Weekend orders dispatch on the next business day and do not begin their 1 business day transit until Monday. Customers must submit time-sensitive orders early in the day to align with warehouse dispatch schedules.

## Are commercial loading-dock deliveries supported for bulk orders?

Commercial loading-dock deliveries are fully supported for bulk orders delivered to verified business addresses during standard operating hours. Business-address delivery hours and specific dock access notes help freight carriers execute seamless unloading procedures. Standard warehouse processing for bulk freight observes the warehouse pick-pack SLA of 24 hours prior to dispatch. Customers should add receiving hours and loading dock specifications directly into the delivery instructions during checkout. Providing accurate commercial facility details ensures freight carriers can accommodate heavy commercial cargo smoothly.

## What should I check before signing for a freight delivery?

Recipients should inspect the pallet and carton exterior thoroughly for visible damage before signing any delivery documentation. Freight shipments require careful external evaluation because signing without notes implies the cargo arrived in good condition. Standard delivery procedures require noting any visible damage directly on the carrier Bill of Lading before the driver departs. Northvale mandates this inspection step to protect freight insurance validity and streamline potential claims. Customers noticing severe structural damage should contact support immediately after documenting the issues on the paperwork.

## How are undeliverable PO Box packages handled upon return?

Undeliverable PO Box packages are handled upon return through standard warehouse intake after the carrier hold expiration period of 7 days lapses. Once the returned item reaches the facility, inventory teams process the incoming shipment and update order statuses accordingly. Customers whose packages return due to postal box restrictions can choose between reshipment to a valid street address or full refund processing. Standard fees such as the expedited shipping fee of $9.95 or next-day shipping fee of $19.95 are evaluated based on the return reason. Support agents assist customers with updating delivery profiles to prevent future addressing errors.
