# Northvale Shipping Lost and Damaged Policy
## How soon must I report a package that arrived damaged?

Customers must report a package that arrived damaged within 48 hours of carrier delivery. Prompt reporting ensures that carrier investigations proceed without unnecessary delays. You need to keep all original packaging materials until the review is fully completed. Discarding boxes or cushioning too early can invalidate the damage claim. Northvale support agents use these materials to verify transit incidents.

## What photos and evidence are needed for a damaged-parcel claim?

A damaged-parcel claim requires clear photographs of the outer box, shipping label, internal cushioning, and item damage. Customers must also provide a photograph showing the serial number for any electronics included in the shipment. Submitting complete photographic evidence allows the claims team to process requests efficiently. Missing photos can delay the verification of the transit incident. You can upload these files directly through the online support portal.

## What should I do if tracking shows delivered but no package arrived?

When tracking shows delivered but no package arrived, customers must immediately check porches, nearby neighbors, and building reception desks. You should also verify the shipping address listed on the original order confirmation. If the package remains missing, a GPS-verified carrier delivery trace request can be initiated. Northvale support reviews delivery scan coordinates to locate the misdelivered parcel. Cooperation from the recipient is mandatory during this trace process.

## When is a police report required for a stolen package claim?

A police report is required for stolen package claims when the total item value exceeds $100. Customers must file this report with local law enforcement and obtain an official incident number. You are required to upload the incident document directly to the support portal for review. Stolen claims below the specified threshold do not necessitate a police report. Northvale verifies all submitted documentation before approving any reshipment.

## What does optional package protection insurance cover?

Optional package protection insurance covers 100% of verified transit loss and damage when added at checkout. The insurance premium is calculated at 1.5% of the total order value. Customers who select this option receive priority processing for replacement orders. Any verified carrier loss is fully reimbursed under this protection plan. You can review the insurance charge itemized on your final checkout screen.

## What is the maximum coverage if I declined shipping insurance?

The uninsured maximum coverage cap is $100 for orders where shipping insurance was declined. Carrier liability limits generally restrict payouts to this baseline amount for unprotected parcels. Customers absorbing the risk of shipping without insurance cannot recover losses exceeding this maximum cap. Northvale applies standard carrier reimbursement rules to all unprotected claims. You are encouraged to select insurance for high-value orders.

## When is a parcel officially considered lost in transit?

A parcel is officially considered lost in transit when tracking remains stalled and a carrier trace is initiated. Transit exception alerts received within 2 hours of a scan update are monitored by logistics systems. Customers must wait for the carrier to complete its internal tracer investigation. Northvale coordinates directly with the courier network to determine final parcel status. Official lost status allows the issuance of replacement orders.

## How does Northvale investigate a lost-in-transit carrier claim?

Northvale investigates a lost-in-transit carrier claim by initiating a formal courier tracer inquiry and verifying warehouse dispatch logs. Customers must cooperate fully during carrier follow-up communications and verification interviews. The logistics team compares dispatch scan records against regional sorting facility logs. This dual verification ensures accurate tracking of missing shipments. Resolution is provided once the carrier confirms the loss.

## How quickly is a replacement order shipped once a claim is approved?

Replacement orders are shipped within 3 business days after a damage or loss claim is formally approved. Customers incur zero shipping cost on all approved replacement dispatches. The replacement order is sent using the original shipping method selected at checkout. You will receive an automated tracking notification as soon as the replacement leaves the warehouse. Northvale expedites these shipments to minimize customer inconvenience.

## Can I choose a refund instead of a replacement for a lost order?

Customers can choose a refund instead of a replacement for a lost order when inventory is low or discontinued. Refunds are settled back to the original payment method used at checkout. The processing time for the refund depends on the financial institution involved. Northvale support agents outline available inventory options during claim approval. You must confirm your preference when the claim is finalized.

## What happens if a carrier holds a parcel after failed delivery attempts?

Carriers hold a parcel for 7 days before returning it to the sender after failed delivery attempts. Customers should contact the local carrier depot before this hold period expires to arrange pickup or redelivery. Unclaimed parcels sent back to Northvale are processed as return-to-sender shipments. You may be responsible for reshipment fees if a parcel returns due to unresponsiveness. Proactive communication with the courier prevents return actions.

## What if only one item in a multi-item box is missing or damaged?

Partial-shipment discrepancies for multi-item boxes are verified against warehouse pack weight logs. Customers missing a single item receive a replacement for that specific piece without needing to return intact items. The claims team cross-references package weight records to confirm dispatch accuracy. You must report the exact missing component during the initial claim submission. Northvale ships the single-item replacement under standard dispatch timelines.

## Should I return a damaged item before my claim is reviewed?

Customers must hold the damaged item and all original packaging until the claim review completes. Northvale issues a prepaid return label only if a physical inspection is specifically required by the quality team. Disposing of the item prematurely violates policy and can result in claim denial. You will receive clear instructions regarding return shipping if an inspection is mandatory. Most claims are resolved using submitted photographs without requiring a physical return.

## What if a lost package eventually arrives after a replacement shipped?

Duplicate packages that eventually arrive after a replacement has already shipped must be returned using a prepaid label. Customers should contact support to coordinate the return of the duplicate parcel. Northvale uses this communication to properly close the active carrier claim file. Keeping both the replacement and the original package is strictly prohibited. Your cooperation ensures inventory accuracy for future orders.

## Are porch-piracy claims covered if the carrier followed delivery instructions?

Porch-piracy claims where the carrier followed delivery instructions are covered only if optional insurance was purchased. Uninsured theft claims are subject to standard carrier liability limits and the $100 maximum coverage cap. Customers filing a stolen package claim exceeding the $100 threshold must provide a valid police report. Insured orders receive full coverage regardless of whether delivery instructions were followed by the courier. Northvale evaluates each theft report against submitted documentation and carrier scan data.
