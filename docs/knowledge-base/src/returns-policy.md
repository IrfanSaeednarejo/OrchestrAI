# Returns Policy

## Return Window

Standard customer returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery. For purchases categorized as electronics and appliances, returns are strictly accepted within 15 days only. Bulk commercial or consumer orders comprising 10 or more units require formal 14-day advance notice submitted to Northvale support before any return initiation can proceed. For seasonal holiday purchases, all qualifying orders placed between November 15 and December 24 receive an extended return window allowing returns through January 31 of the following year. In all circumstances, the official eligibility window starts the day after carrier delivery confirmation rather than the original order placement date.

## Item Condition Requirements

All returned items must arrive unworn, unwashed, and with all original manufacturer tags firmly attached. Items that arrive damaged, altered, or laundered fail standard intake inspection. However, demonstrably defective items are completely exempt from standard condition requirements and will be received regardless of wear or packaging status. Damaged outer shipping packaging is fully acceptable during intake provided the underlying product itself remains intact and undamaged. Sealed consumable goods must remain completely unopened with the manufacturer security seal intact to qualify for return. Any approved returns arriving with missing components or original accessories will incur a mandatory deduction of up to 25% from the final refund amount.

## Return Shipping Costs

Northvale provides complimentary free return shipping for all verified defective items or products shipped incorrectly due to fulfillment errors. When a customer initiates a standard change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for all associated return shipping costs. The standardized cost for return shipping is established at $6.95 for the first item, plus an additional $2.50 for each subsequent item in the shipment. A prepaid return shipping label can be accessed directly through the online account portal within 24 hours of submitting the return request. The calculated return shipping cost is deducted directly from the final issued refund balance rather than charged upfront at label creation.

## Refund Method and Timeline

All approved monetary refunds are issued exclusively to the customer's original payment method without exception. Standard refund processing requires 5 to 7 business days following verified receipt and physical intake inspection of the return at the fulfillment facility. Original order shipping charges remain non-refundable in all standard return cases unless the underlying returned item was officially confirmed defective. Returns originating from gift purchases are non-refundable to payment cards and are issued strictly as store credit. For loss prevention and security compliance, any returned package carrying a value exceeding $500 requires signature-verified receipt at the intake dock before inspection begins.

## Exceptions and Non-Returnable Items

Perishable goods, physical and digital gift cards, and all digital downloads are strictly non-returnable once purchased. Custom-made or personalized merchandise becomes completely non-returnable once manufacturing or customization production begins. All personal underwear and swimwear garments are non-returnable due to mandatory health and hygiene regulations. Any returned merchandise received at the intake warehouse after the expiration of the return window is accepted only at Northvale's discretion and is subject to a 50% restocking fee. Items designated as final sale are explicitly marked at checkout and cannot be returned under any circumstances.
