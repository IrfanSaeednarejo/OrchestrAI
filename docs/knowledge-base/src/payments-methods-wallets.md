# Digital Wallets and Alternative Payments Policy
## Which digital wallets are accepted at Northvale checkout?

Northvale accepts Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal as valid digital wallet payment methods at checkout. Availability of these specific digital wallets depends on the customer browsing device and the active browser application. Customers using compatible devices will automatically see the corresponding wallet buttons on the payment screen. Selecting a digital wallet bypasses standard manual card entry by utilizing stored credentials. Each supported wallet integrates directly with the secure checkout infrastructure to process transactions.

## How does Express Checkout work with Apple Pay and Google Pay?

Express Checkout using Apple Pay and Google Pay automatically populates pre-filled shipping and billing details directly from the wallet profile. Customers must carefully verify their default shipping address and contact information within the wallet interface before authorizing the final purchase. This verification ensures that the order routes to the correct delivery destination without requiring manual form completion. The system instantly transmits the selected delivery and payment parameters to finalize the transaction securely. Customers can update their default profile settings inside their respective device wallet applications at any time.

## When do digital wallet payments require extra security verification?

Digital wallet payments require extra security verification whenever a transaction reaches the digital wallet step-up threshold of $500 or exceeds the 3-D Secure challenge threshold of $250. This additional verification aligns directly with standard 3D Secure protocols designed to protect accounts against unauthorized use. Customers may be prompted by their device or card issuer to complete biometric confirmation or enter a one-time passcode. Failure to complete the mandatory step-up challenge will result in transaction cancellation. The security system evaluates risk parameters dynamically for every single purchase attempt.

## How does Klarna Buy Now Pay Later work at checkout?

Klarna Buy Now Pay Later operates at checkout for qualifying orders that fall within the strict eligible order range of $50 to $2,000. Customers select Klarna as their payment method to review and choose from available interest-free installment schedule options. The total purchase amount divides into equal scheduled payments managed through the Klarna platform. Northvale captures the initial installment at the time of order confirmation and fulfillment. Subsequent payments are automatically debited according to the agreed schedule chosen by the customer.

## Why might Klarna be unavailable for my cart?

Klarna might be unavailable for a specific cart if the order total falls below the minimum limit or exceeds the maximum limit specified by the eligible order range of $50 to $2,000. Additionally, carts containing recurring subscriptions or restricted-item exclusions automatically disqualify the entire transaction from utilizing Buy Now Pay Later services. Internal risk assessments or credit evaluations conducted by the provider can also restrict availability for specific accounts. Customers encountering this restriction must select an alternative accepted payment method to complete the purchase. Reviewing cart contents for ineligible items often resolves the payment option availability issue.

## Can I save or vault a digital wallet for future one-click orders?

Northvale supports tokenized wallet vaulting rather than storing raw card numbers for future one-click orders. This vaulting mechanism relies on secure security tokens bound strictly to the customer account profile. Per-session biometric authentication may still be required depending on device settings and transaction risk parameters. Vaulted tokens ensure that sensitive payment credentials remain encrypted and inaccessible to unauthorized parties. Customers maintain full control over their saved payment tokens through their account settings dashboard.

## How fast are PayPal refunds processed compared to cards?

PayPal refunds are processed within a standard timeframe of 48 hours following the formal approval of a return request. This timeline is significantly faster than standard card refund processing timelines, which typically require multiple business days to post. The refunded funds return directly to the original PayPal balance or linked funding source associated with the transaction. Northvale initiates the refund immediately upon completing the warehouse return inspection. Customers receive an automated electronic notification once the refund transmission is successfully completed.

## How are Klarna refunds handled if I return an order?

Klarna refunds are handled by automatically canceling any remaining unpaid installments and refunding settled installments within 5 business days. The Klarna app balance updates automatically to reflect the adjusted payment schedule or credit balance once Northvale processes the return. Customers who paid installments prior to the return approval receive the corresponding credit directly from the provider. Any remaining balance adjustments appear in the customer account dashboard managed by the third-party service. This automated process ensures that customers are only charged for items kept from the order.

## Can I combine a digital wallet with Klarna on one order?

Customers cannot combine a digital wallet with Klarna or any other Buy Now Pay Later service on a single order. The checkout system strictly enforces a single primary payment rail rule for all wallet and BNPL transactions. Splitting a single transaction across multiple competing wallet or installment providers is technically unsupported. Customers must choose one exclusive payment method to cover the entire order balance at checkout. Selecting a primary payment rail prevents authorization errors and ensures smooth order processing.

## Why did my Apple Pay or Google Pay transaction fail?

Apple Pay or Google Pay transactions typically fail due to an expired underlying card, a billing ZIP code mismatch, or a step-up authentication timeout exceeding the threshold of $500 or $250. Customers experiencing these specific failures should update their active card details inside their native device wallet application. Ensuring that the billing address matches the financial institution records prevents automated fraud prevention rejections. Device connectivity issues can also interrupt the secure token transmission during checkout. Refreshing the browser session and re-initiating the wallet payment often resolves temporary processing blocks.

## Does PayPal Pay in 4 follow the same rules as Klarna?

PayPal Pay in 4 financing eligibility is governed entirely by PayPal terms presented at the time of user login rather than Northvale internal policies. Northvale receives full payment settlement at the moment of capture regardless of the customer installment selection. This means that alternative financing structures offered through PayPal operate independently from standard store-managed BNPL rules. Customers must review PayPal account agreements for specific terms regarding late fees and repayment schedules. The broader order restrictions still apply to all transactions processed through external financing rails.

## Are digital wallet credentials stored on Northvale servers?

Digital wallet credentials are never stored as raw card numbers on Northvale servers under any operational circumstances. The system relies exclusively on tokenized vaulting mechanisms that utilize device-bound cryptogram security to process transactions. These cryptographic tokens protect sensitive financial data from exposure during data transmission and storage. Third-party payment gateways manage the underlying vault infrastructure in compliance with strict industry security standards. Customer account profiles retain only the encrypted reference tokens necessary for identifying preferred payment options.

## Can I switch from a digital wallet to a card after checkout?

Northvale strictly prohibits post-checkout payment method swaps once an order has been successfully authorized and submitted. Customers wishing to change their payment instrument must cancel the existing order within the designated cutoff window and place a new order using the preferred card. This restriction maintains accounting integrity and prevents discrepancies in transaction authorization logs. Customer service agents cannot manually alter the payment rail of a finalized order. Reviewing the payment selection carefully prior to final authorization avoids the need for cancellation and reordering.

## Do digital wallets support international currency checkout?

Digital wallets support multi-currency checkout matching the specific local currency presented at the Northvale store checkout. The underlying bank and card issuer foreign exchange rules still apply to all cross-border digital wallet transactions. Customers utilizing international payment methods may incur conversion fees determined by their financial institution. The checkout interface displays the final transaction amount in the local currency before the customer authorizes payment. Wallet providers handle the currency routing transparently based on the linked funding source parameters.

## How do I remove a linked PayPal or wallet token from my account?

Linked PayPal accounts and digital wallet tokens are removed by managing saved payment methods directly within the account settings dashboard. Accessing the payment management section allows customers to unlink associated tokens with a single action. The system executes an immediate token revocation that permanently severs the connection to the Northvale profile. Future checkouts will no longer display the revoked wallet option as a quick payment choice. Customers can add new or updated payment tokens at any time through the same account management interface.
