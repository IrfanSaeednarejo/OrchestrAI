# Privacy Policy Summary

## Data We Collect

Northvale collects necessary account data during profile creation and checkout, including your full name, email address, telephone contact numbers, and designated shipping addresses. We maintain complete order data comprising itemized lists of products purchased, tokenized payment details received from our payment processor, and carrier delivery tracking histories. When you access our digital storefront, device telemetry is collected, including internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser specifications, operating system versions, and unique hardware identifiers. We also record general usage metrics, such as pages visited, catalog search queries, and logged customer support interactions. Northvale does not collect biometric data or government-issued identification cards except when strictly necessary for formal identity verification.

## How We Use Your Data

Collected user information is utilized primarily to facilitate reliable order fulfillment, processing, and domestic and international delivery coordination. We rely on personal contact and transaction histories to administer customer support inquiries, evaluate warranty claims, and investigate disputed charges. Personal data is essential for our automated fraud-prevention systems, protecting customer profiles, preventing account takeover attempts, and enforcing overall platform security. Furthermore, browsing data enables product recommendation algorithms and customized shopping experiences tailored to user preferences. Finally, Northvale delivers promotional marketing communications and product news exclusively to customers who have provided explicit affirmative consent through opt-in channels.

## Data Sharing

Northvale shares customer information with trusted third-party fulfillment partners, including commercial shipping carriers, payment processing gateways, and specialized fraud-prevention service providers. We also share platform traffic analytics with verified measurement partners exclusively in aggregated, de-identified formats that cannot identify individual users. Northvale never sells personal customer information to independent third parties for their own commercial advertising or marketing campaigns. We disclose confidential account records to government agencies or law enforcement authorities only when compelled by valid legal process, such as subpoenas or judicial warrants. In the event of a business merger, acquisition, or asset sale, customer records are transferred under these same strict confidentiality terms.

## Data Retention

Account profile records and active login credentials are retained throughout the active operational lifespan of your Northvale user account. Transactional order data, including itemized receipts and billing histories, is retained for a period of 7 years in strict compliance with federal tax regulations and statutory accounting standards. Customer service records, chat transcripts, and recorded support tickets are retained for 3 years to ensure support quality and resolve recurring customer inquiries. Digital marketing telemetry and advertising interaction records are retained for 2 years following collection. Finally, fully anonymized statistical data and aggregated traffic logs are retained indefinitely for longitudinal system analysis.

## Your Privacy Rights

Customers maintain the legal right to access, inspect, correct inaccurate information, or request the permanent deletion of their personal data from our systems. You retain the right to withdraw marketing consent and opt out of promotional communications at any time without penalty or loss of service. Customers may exercise data portability rights by requesting an export of their personal profile and transaction history delivered in standardized CSV or JSON formats. Residents of California enjoy comprehensive protections under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the explicit right to prohibit the sale of personal information. Similarly, individuals in the European Union and United Kingdom may exercise General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) rights.

## Children's Privacy

Registration of a Northvale account requires individuals to be at least 18 years of age or the legal age of majority in their jurisdiction. Northvale does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 under any circumstances. In the event our compliance team discovers an account registered to a minor under 13, the account profile and associated personal data are permanently deleted within 5 business days. For adolescent users between 13 and 17 years of age, verified parental consent is strictly required in jurisdictions where local privacy legislation mandates it. Full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) is rigorously enforced through our age verification protocols.
