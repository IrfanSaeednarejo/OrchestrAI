# Store Credit, Refunds, and Vouchers Policy
## When are refunds issued as store credit instead of cash?

Refunds are issued as store credit instead of cash for specific gift returns and expired or closed account fallback cases at Northvale. Standard returns typically route back to the original payment method used during checkout. However, system exceptions require alternative credit pathways when direct card crediting fails. Customers must review account notifications to determine the exact issuance method chosen for each transaction. Northvale applies these rules uniformly to protect transaction security and account integrity.

## How are returns from gift purchases refunded?

Returns from gift purchases are refunded strictly as store credit to the recipient account. The original buyer payment card is never credited under any gift return circumstances. This policy ensures that gift recipients retain the full value of returned items without exposing buyer payment details. Northvale processes these designated gift transactions through the customer service portal upon receipt. Gift return credits automatically bind to the recipient email address provided during the return initiation.

## Do Northvale store credits ever expire?

Northvale store credits never expire and remain permanently bound to the customer account wallet. Customers can check their current store credit balance at any time by visiting their account settings dashboard. This non-expiration rule applies to all store credits issued through returns, promotional adjustments, or customer service accommodations. Northvale maintains these funds securely within the primary digital wallet until the customer applies them to a purchase. Account holders should verify their active balances before completing online checkouts.

## Can store credit be transferred to another customer account?

Store credit is completely non-transferable and remains strictly bound to the recipient email address. Customers cannot move store credit balances between separate accounts or assign funds to other individuals. Northvale enforces this restriction to prevent unauthorized transfers and maintain strict account security protocols. Every store credit balance stays linked to the specific profile that processed the original return or customer service claim. Customers must utilize their assigned credits exclusively within their own registered accounts.

## What happens to promotional vouchers when an order is refunded?

Promotional vouchers undergo single-use coupon forfeiture or percentage promo recalculation when an order is refunded. The original voucher value is never refunded as cash or converted into withdrawable funds by Northvale. Promotional discounts applied to returned items are permanently lost upon order cancellation. Customers retain only the standard monetary or credit value associated with the items actually kept. Northvale calculates these adjustments automatically when processing the return through the system.

## How are loyalty points or rewards adjusted after a refund?

Loyalty points earned on refunded items are systematically deducted from the customer rewards balance. Redeemed reward value returns directly to the customer account as store credit rather than cash. Northvale adjusts all associated rewards immediately upon final inspection and return approval. Customers can view their updated loyalty tier and point totals within the rewards section of their profile. These adjustments ensure that reward distribution aligns strictly with completed purchase totals.

## Can I convert a monetary refund to store credit voluntarily?

Standard refunds default to the original payment method used during the initial purchase transaction. Customers may select an optional store credit conversion when prompted inside the return portal interface. Northvale provides this voluntary store credit choice to facilitate faster future checkouts for frequent buyers. Once a customer confirms the store credit selection, the choice becomes final and irreversible. Monetary refunds already routed to a banking institution cannot be recalled for credit conversion.

## What happens if my original bank account is permanently closed?

Rejected refunds resulting from closed bank accounts bounce back to the merchant administration system. Northvale confirms fallback issuance as store credit after verifying the failed transfer status. Customers receive an automated notification alerting them to the updated credit placement in their account wallet. This fallback mechanism ensures that funds from unroutable payments remain fully accessible for future purchases. Customers must maintain accurate banking details to minimize settlement delays on standard returns.

## Within what window must a gift return be initiated for store credit?

Gift returns for store credit must be initiated within the standard return window of 30 days. Condition requirements still apply strictly to all eligible gift items submitted for evaluation. Northvale rejects any gift return requests submitted outside this designated timeframe. Gift recipients must ensure that items arrive at the processing facility within the proper operational period. The standard return window begins on the original delivery date recorded by the carrier.

## What if a gift return fails baseline warehouse inspection?

Gift returns failing baseline warehouse inspection receive an adjusted refund rate of 60%. This adjusted amount issues exclusively as store credit to the recipient account wallet. Northvale applies this reduction when returned merchandise shows signs of excessive wear or missing components. Customers are notified of the inspection failure and the resulting credit valuation via email. The remaining value of the uninspected merchandise is permanently forfeited.

## How quickly is store credit available after return approval?

Store credit becomes available instantly in the account wallet upon final return approval. This rapid posting occurs within the standard refund processing window of 5 to 7 business days. Northvale issues a confirmation email containing the updated credit balance immediately after processing finishes. Customers can apply the newly posted store credit to eligible orders right away. System verification delays rarely extend past the stated business day benchmark.

## Can store credit be used toward shipping fees and taxes?

Store credit applies directly toward the subtotal, shipping fees, and taxes at checkout. Any remaining balance stays securely stored in the customer account wallet for future purchases. Northvale deducts the necessary credit amount automatically when the customer selects store credit as the payment method. Customers can combine store credit with other accepted payment types if the order total exceeds the available balance. This flexible application applies to all standard product categories across the platform.

## How do I dispute an incorrect store credit amount?

Customers must file a dispute within the 60-day dispute filing window from the refund issue date. Discrepancies should be reported directly to Northvale support with the corresponding order and return IDs. Northvale reviews the transaction history and warehouse logs to verify the correct credit amount. Submitting incomplete reference numbers will delay the investigation of the reported credit error. Resolved disputes result in an automatic balance adjustment within the customer wallet.

## Can store credit be redeemed for cash or card payout?

Store credit cannot be redeemed for cash or card payout under any standard operating circumstance. This non-cash policy remains strictly enforced across all account types and transaction histories. Northvale prohibits cash redemptions except in specific jurisdictions where required by applicable state law. Customers must spend their store credit balances on platform purchases rather than requesting liquid payouts. Support agents cannot override this restriction for standard customer accounts.

## What happens to store credit if I delete my Northvale account?

Permanent account deletion forfeits any unspent store credit remaining in the digital wallet. Customers are strongly advised to use their store credit balances before submitting account closure requests. Northvale cannot recover or reissue forfeited store credit after an account deletion is finalized. Account termination permanently purges all associated wallet funds and transaction histories from the active database. Users should verify their remaining balances prior to initiating profile removal.
