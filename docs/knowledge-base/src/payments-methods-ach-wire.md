# Northvale ACH and Wire Transfer Payment Policy
## When can I pay using an electronic ACH bank transfer?

Customers may pay using an electronic ACH bank transfer when orders exceed the minimum threshold of $200. This payment rail requires a valid US checking or savings bank account operated by the customer. Northvale processes these transactions securely through established automated clearing house networks. Transactions of this type remain subject to standard institutional verification checks. The checking account must possess sufficient funds to cover the complete order amount at the time of purchase.

## How do I verify and link my bank account for ACH checkout?

Customers verify and link bank accounts for ACH checkout through instant bank verification or the micro-deposit flow. Northvale utilizes encrypted bank token handling to protect all sensitive financial data transmitted during linking. The system securely stores these payment tokens to facilitate future authorized purchases. Institutional credentials are never permanently stored on Northvale servers under any circumstances. Customers complete authentication directly within the secure checkout interface before submitting orders.

## How long does an ACH transfer take to clear before my order ships?

An ACH transfer takes 3 business days to clear before an order ships. The Northvale warehouse maintains a strict pick-pack wait policy until funds fully clear the banking network. Orders remain on a temporary fulfillment hold during this mandatory clearing duration. Customers receive an automated shipment confirmation once the settlement process concludes successfully. This timeline ensures banking institutions finalize the transfer before physical inventory leaves the facility.

## What happens if an ACH transfer fails due to insufficient funds?

Northvale applies a returned transfer fee of $15 when an ACH transfer fails due to insufficient funds. The associated order experiences an immediate administrative hold or cancellation following the returned transfer notification. Customers must settle any outstanding NSF fees before placing new orders using electronic payment rails. The returned item notice triggers automated communication regarding the failed transaction status. Resolving the fee allows customers to select alternative valid payment methods for desired purchases.

## When are direct domestic wire transfers accepted?

Direct domestic wire transfers are accepted when orders exceed the minimum order threshold of $5,000. This high-value payment option serves commercial accounts and large wholesale purchase requirements efficiently. Northvale designates wire transfers specifically for qualifying orders that surpass standard electronic transaction ceilings. Customers arranging large commercial shipments coordinate directly through this specialized institutional rail. The threshold ensures appropriate handling of substantial financial obligations.

## What administrative fee applies to domestic wire transfers?

Northvale assesses a $25 wire processing fee as an administrative charge applied to domestic wire transfers. Sending-bank wire fees charged by originating financial institutions remain entirely separate from this Northvale administrative fee. Customers must ensure that their originating bank covers any intermediary wire transfer costs independently. The total wire transmission sent should account for all external bank fees to prevent short-pay discrepancies. This administrative fee covers the internal reconciliation of direct wire payments.

## How do I obtain Northvale’s wire instructions and reference memo?

Customers obtain Northvale wire instructions and reference memos through the pro-forma invoice generation feature at checkout. The generated pro-forma document provides precise routing and account details required by the sending bank. Customers must include the unique order ID within the wire memo field for accurate payment reconciliation. Omitting the order ID delays the manual matching process required to release the fulfillment hold. Financial teams use this reference memo to link incoming funds to the correct transaction.

## Can I split payment between a bank transfer and a credit card?

Customers cannot split payments between a bank transfer and a credit card due to single-rail transaction restrictions. Split-tender rules apply exclusively to combinations involving standard cards and digital wallets. Alternative rails such as ACH and wire transfers must cover the entire invoice in a single transaction. Each order utilizing electronic bank rails requires a unified payment method for accurate financial tracking. Customers choosing wire transfers must remit the exact invoice balance through that single transfer.

## How long do ACH refunds take when an order is cancelled or returned?

ACH refunds take 3 business days to settle when an order is cancelled or returned. Northvale issues this refund directly back to the originating bank account used during the initial purchase. The processing timeline begins immediately after the return inspection or cancellation request gains approval. Banking institutions may require additional posting time before the credited funds appear in the account. Customers receive electronic notification confirming the refund transmission to the bank.

## Can expedited or next-day shipping be used with ACH payments?

Expedited or next-day shipping transit speeds apply only after the underlying ACH payment successfully clears. The standard clearing delay affects urgent delivery expectations because fulfillment waits for settled funds. Selecting expedited transit upgrades the shipping speed once the 3 business days settlement window concludes. Customers requiring immediate delivery should consider alternative non-ACH payment methods to avoid pre-shipment delays. Inventory allocation remains reserved during the clearing period despite the delayed warehouse release.

## What happens if a wire transfer arrives with an incorrect amount?

Northvale places a short-pay hold on orders when a wire transfer arrives below the required invoice total. Over-payments trigger a partial refund process to return the excess funds to the sender. Customers are strongly advised to verify all wire fee inclusions before sending funds from their bank. Any short-pay variance requires an additional secondary wire transfer to cover the remaining balance. Resolving the precise amount ensures timely order processing without administrative intervention.

## Are international wire transfers supported for overseas commercial buyers?

International wire transfers are currently restricted due to policies supporting exclusively domestic USD wire transactions. Overseas commercial buyers cannot utilize standard international SWIFT rails for purchases on the platform. International commercial buyers should direct inquiries regarding alternative arrangements to billing support personnel. Northvale maintains strict routing limitations for cross-border financial transactions to ensure regulatory compliance. Domestic banking accounts remain a mandatory prerequisite for processing direct wire payments.

## How long do I have to send a wire transfer after placing an order?

Customers have a specific invoice expiration window to send a wire transfer before unallocated inventory is released. Placing a wire order initiates a temporary hold on the requested merchandise for a limited duration. Failing to transmit the wire funds within the designated timeframe causes the system to cancel the order. Customers should email their wire confirmation reference to support immediately after sending the transfer. Prompt notification prevents automated inventory release and ensures smooth order processing.

## Can I save a verified bank account for repeat ACH orders?

Customers can save a verified bank account for repeat ACH orders through the saved ACH mandate settings. Account payment settings allow users to store authorized banking profiles for future streamlined checkouts. The saved ACH mandate authorizes Northvale to debit the linked account for subsequent approved purchases. Customers retain the right to revoke this bank mandate anytime directly within their account settings. Revoking the mandate removes the stored banking information from the active profile immediately.

## How are wire transfer refunds processed?

Wire transfer refunds are processed using a verified bank-account return wire or a standard ACH credit. Northvale initiates this refund after the intake and return approval process concludes successfully. The finance department coordinates directly with the customer to confirm receiving account details. Processing times reflect standard institutional transfer speeds following the initial internal approval. Customers receive detailed confirmation once the refund transaction is transmitted to the bank.
