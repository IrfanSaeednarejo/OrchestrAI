# Northvale Orders Cancellations and Address Edits Policy
## Can I edit my shipping address after placing an order?

Customers can edit a shipping address after placing an order by accessing the self-service address edit button in the order details during the brief post-checkout window. This modification window remains open until the warehouse pick-pack process begins for the specific shipment. Northvale provides this digital tool to ensure accurate routing before physical fulfillment commences. Once fulfillment tasks initiate, electronic edits are automatically disabled to maintain operational consistency. Customers must then rely on alternative support workflows if further modifications are required.

## When does the warehouse pick-pack cutoff lock order edits?

The warehouse pick-pack cutoff locks order edits exactly 24 hours after order placement, which aligns with the standard warehouse pick-pack sla of 24 hours. Following this initial phase, the warehouse processing-to-ship window spans 24 to 48 hours for final carrier dispatch. Because printed shipping labels cannot be edited locally within the facility, all automated and manual order modifications are strictly prohibited after this milestone. Northvale enforces this cutoff to prevent mislabeling and logistical delays in the fulfillment chain. Customers attempting changes past this threshold must review alternative delivery management options.

## Can I cancel an order if the address edit window has closed?

Customers can cancel an order if the address edit window has closed only if the request occurs within the standard cancellation cutoff of 24 hours. Northvale applies this cancellation limit to maintain efficiency across all active fulfillment operations. If the order has progressed beyond this timeframe, direct cancellation is no longer supported by the system. Customers must instead wait for parcel delivery and utilize standard return procedures if needed. Support agents cannot bypass these automated operational cutoffs once fulfillment is underway.

## How do I fix a typo in my street number or apartment unit?

Minor unit fixes for street numbers or apartment suites can be addressed by submitting a direct support request before the carrier handoff occurs. Major changes like city, state, or ZIP code alterations are treated differently due to regulatory and tax requirements. Northvale support agents evaluate minor correction requests on a case-by-case basis prior to dispatch. If the shipment has already reached the carrier handoff stage, these corrections can no longer be applied manually. Customers must monitor tracking updates closely once the package enters the transit network.

## Can I change the shipping tier after checkout?

Customers cannot upgrade or downgrade the shipping speed post-authorization once an order has been successfully completed at checkout. Northvale systems do not permit mid-stream modifications to selected transit tiers after payment processing concludes. Customers who require a different delivery speed must cancel the original transaction within the standard cancellation cutoff of 24 hours and place a new order. This ensures correct freight calculations and accurate billing alignment for the chosen service level. Separate transactions cannot be merged or retroactively adjusted after submission.

## Can I remove a single item from a recently placed order?

Customers can remove a single item from a recently placed order provided the request happens before the pick-pack lock takes effect. Northvale processes partial item cancellations immediately upon receipt through the customer account portal. This action automatically triggers the refund initiation protocol within 2 hours for the removed merchandise. Items that have already entered the packaging stage cannot be separated from the active shipment. Customers must handle any unwanted delivered goods through standard return channels.

## Can I add extra items to an existing order after checkout?

Customers cannot add extra items to an existing payment authorization once checkout has been finalized. Northvale financial security rules prohibit modifying closed transaction totals to include additional merchandise. Customers desiring extra products must place a separate, independent order through the standard storefront interface. This separate transaction will generate its own fulfillment schedule and associated delivery terms. Combining distinct orders into a single shipment is not permitted by warehouse logistics.

## What happens if my parcel already shipped to an old address?

Parcels that have already shipped to an old address are subject to carrier package-intercept limitations once officially in transit. Northvale activates shipping tracking activation sla milestones within 4 hours of carrier pickup to provide visibility. Customers can attempt carrier redirect options directly through the external shipping provider portal if the carrier supports such requests. If the carrier denies the redirect, the package will typically proceed to the original destination address. Northvale assumes no financial liability for parcels successfully delivered to user-submitted addresses.

## Are carrier address-intercept requests guaranteed to succeed?

Carrier address-intercept requests are never guaranteed to succeed because success depends entirely on carrier hub scans and specific route eligibility. Northvale cannot override external carrier network policies regarding mid-route redirection attempts. If an intercept attempt fails, the parcel automatically falls back to a return-to-sender workflow governed by the carrier. Customers will be notified once the physical item begins its return journey back to the fulfillment center. Subsequent reshipment or refund processing will commence after warehouse intake is complete.

## Can I change a residential address to a PO Box after ordering?

Customers cannot switch a street address to a PO Box after ordering if a private courier has already been assigned to the fulfillment route. Northvale restricts PO Box modifications post-checkout to comply with specific carrier acceptance criteria and delivery limitations. Customers who need a PO Box destination must cancel the original transaction before dispatch and submit a new order. This cancel-and-reorder workflow prevents delivery exceptions and carrier rejection of the parcel. Support teams cannot manually reroute private courier shipments to post office boxes.

## How do gift message or recipient name edits work after checkout?

Gift messages and recipient name edits are managed through support-assisted updates prior to the initiation of warehouse pick-pack operations. Northvale freezes the packing slip data permanently once warehouse personnel begin picking items for the order. Customers must contact support immediately to request these specific text updates within the initial processing window. Once the packing slip is printed and attached, no further document alterations are permitted. Unedited documents will accompany parcels shipped past this operational milestone.

## What if I cancel an order within cutoff to fix my address?

Canceling an order within the standard cancellation cutoff of 24 hours to fix an address immediately enables the refund initiation after cancel process within 2 hours. Northvale automated systems process these cancellations instantly to release held funds back to the original payment method. Customers gain the immediate ability to place a corrected order with accurate shipping details right away. This rapid workflow ensures that inventory is released and customer intent is accurately captured without delay. No financial penalties apply to cancellations executed properly within the stated timeframe.

## What happens if an undeliverable wrong-address parcel comes back?

Undeliverable wrong-address parcels that return to the facility undergo warehouse intake and prompt customer notification upon arrival. Northvale processes these returned items according to standard disposition guidelines for undeliverable freight. Customers can then choose between eligible refund options or a new reshipment after updating their address data in the system. Original shipping fees may be deducted from the final refund amount depending on the carrier return reason code. Customers must verify all destination fields carefully before initiating any replacement orders.

## How does standard return eligibility apply if interception fails?

Standard return eligibility applies once delivered when a package intercept fails and the wrong-address parcel arrives at the destination. Northvale enforces a standard return window of 30 days starting from the official delivery date recorded by the carrier. Customers may choose either refusal-at-door methods where applicable or process a standard return through the online portal. Returned items must meet all baseline condition requirements before any credit is issued to the buyer. Return shipping fees may apply in accordance with standard policy terms.

## Why are cross-state or international address changes blocked?

Cross-state or international address changes are blocked automatically due to mandatory sales tax recalculation rules and strict fraud-prevention locks. Northvale compliance systems prevent partial edits across jurisdictional borders to maintain accurate fiscal reporting and security standards. Customers requiring cross-state or international destination changes must perform a full cancellation and complete a new checkout. This ensures proper tax assessment and authorized payment verification for the new destination country or state. Support agents cannot manually override these jurisdictional security locks under any circumstances.
