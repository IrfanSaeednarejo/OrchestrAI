# Northvale Account Deletion and Erasure Policy
## How do I submit a self-service account deletion request?

Customers can submit a self-service account deletion request by navigating to Account Settings and selecting the Delete Account workflow. This digital pathway guides users through the necessary steps to initiate profile removal without requiring direct agent intervention. Alternatively, customers may also contact Northvale support to request a support-assisted deletion if they experience technical issues. The system immediately registers the request once the online submission process is successfully completed by the user. Subsequent verification steps must then be fulfilled to validate the identity of the account holder before the workflow proceeds.

## What identity verification is required before deleting my account?

Northvale requires account password confirmation and an OTP verification step before deleting any customer account. Users must enter their current account password alongside a unique security code to prove authorized access. The generated OTP remains active for a strict duration of exactly 10 minutes. If the user fails to input the code within this timeframe, a new verification code must be requested. This mandatory security measure prevents unauthorized third parties from maliciously terminating customer profiles.

## How quickly will I receive confirmation of my deletion request?

Customers will receive a formal deletion confirmation email within a strict SLA of 24 hours after submitting a request. This automated notification serves as official acknowledgment that the account profile has entered the deletion pipeline. Users should thoroughly check their registered email inbox and associated spam filters for this documentation. The message outlines the timeline of the deletion process and provides instructions for any necessary follow-up actions. Retention of this confirmation receipt is recommended until the permanent erasure procedure fully concludes.

## What is the grace period before permanent deletion executes?

Northvale enforces a mandatory deletion grace period of exactly 30 days before permanent database erasure executes. During this duration, the target account enters an inactive state where standard purchasing capabilities and portal features are suspended. This window allows customers sufficient time to reconsider their decision or recover important historical documentation if needed. No permanent data purging occurs until this 30-day period has completely elapsed across all operational servers. The account remains recoverable exclusively through designated authentication workflows during this active temporal window.

## How can I cancel a deletion request and reactivate my account?

Customers can cancel an active deletion request and reactivate their account by signing back in with their email and password during the grace period. Successful authentication immediately halts the scheduled deletion workflow and restores the profile to standard operational status. Northvale confirms full restoration of all account settings, saved preferences, and historical data upon successful reactivation. Users do not need to contact customer support to reverse the pending deletion request if they access the platform prior to the deadline. Once the reactivation is complete, normal e-commerce functions become instantly accessible to the user again.

## How fast does permanent database deletion run once the grace period ends?

Permanent database deletion runs within a strict SLA of 48 hours once the grace period concludes. This automated purge operation removes user profile records, addresses, and transaction metadata across all operational systems. Northvale executes these routines to ensure complete and irreversible removal of customer data from active production databases. The technical process runs without manual intervention once the 30-day holding window officially expires. Users cannot reverse or halt the erasure procedure once this 48-hour execution window initiates.

## How long does it take for encrypted backups to purge my deleted data?

Encrypted backups purge deleted customer data through a scheduled archive purge cycle of exactly 30 days. Automated snapshot rotation protocols systematically overwrite historical backup files containing the residual user records. Northvale coordinates these backup lifecycle policies to align with standard data protection compliance mandates. Because backups operate on rotating schedules, complete erasure across offline archives matches this designated purge duration. This final technical step ensures that no lingering copies of the deleted profile remain in secure storage repositories.

## What happens to saved payment methods and addresses after deletion?

Saved payment methods and shipping addresses undergo permanent removal from Northvale systems following account deletion. The platform strips all stored credit card tokens, billing profiles, and delivery locations from the database during the purge cycle. Customers are explicitly informed that these payment credentials and addresses cannot be recovered under any circumstances. Any future purchases on Northvale require manual re-entry of payment details and delivery destinations from scratch. This strict removal protocol protects sensitive financial data from unauthorized retention or exposure.

## What happens to unused store credit or loyalty points upon deletion?

Unspent store credits and loyalty point balances are permanently forfeited upon the completion of account deletion. Northvale advises customers to redeem all remaining financial balances and reward perks before submitting a deletion request. Once the account purge procedure executes, loyalty tiers and promotional credits cannot be reinstated or transferred. The automated deletion system zeroes out all remaining balances without providing monetary refunds for unspent promotional funds. Careful review of account assets is strongly recommended prior to initiating the self-service erasure workflow.

## Can I create a new account with the same email after permanent deletion?

Re-registration with the same email address is fully allowed after the permanent purge process completes. Northvale permits former users to establish a fresh customer profile using previously registered credentials once data deletion is finalized. However, no historical orders, loyalty points, store credits, or past settings carry over to the newly created account. The new profile starts completely blank as an independent entity within the e-commerce database. Customers must verify their email address again to activate the newly established secondary account.

## Will I receive a final email confirming permanent erasure?

Northvale dispatches a final automated erasure confirmation notice to verify that the permanent deletion process has concluded. This message informs the former user that all account records and associated personal data have been wiped from operational systems. The associated email address is purged from the database immediately after dispatch of this final notification. Consequently, customers will not receive any further correspondence from Northvale regarding the erased account profile. This communication acts as the definitive closing record for the data deletion lifecycle.

## What happens to active marketing subscriptions when I delete my account?

Active marketing subscriptions undergo automatic removal from all promotional email and SMS distribution lists when an account is deleted. Northvale updates suppression databases to ensure zero post-deletion promotional messages reach the former customer. This unsubscription process applies universally to newsletters, discount alerts, and targeted marketing campaigns. Customers do not need to manage individual subscription preferences separately prior to initiating account erasure. The system guarantees that marketing communications cease permanently alongside the data removal workflow.

## Can I download my order history before my account is purged?

Customers should download PDF invoices or request a Data Subject Access Request export before the grace period ends. Northvale locks the self-service portal during the inactive grace state, which prevents direct transactional downloads once the closure process initiates. Users must proactively retrieve their historical purchase records prior to submitting the initial deletion request. Support teams cannot generate retrospective invoice packages after the account has entered the final purge queue. Maintaining personal copies of past receipts ensures proper record-keeping for personal accounting purposes.

## What if I cannot remember my password to cancel during the grace period?

Customers who forget their password during the grace period must use the Forgot Password recovery flow within the active window. This recovery mechanism enables users to reset their credentials and regain portal access to halt the pending deletion. Northvale enforces strict security protocols, meaning support agents cannot manually bypass authentication to cancel deletions. If the user fails to complete password recovery before the 30-day timeline expires, permanent erasure executes automatically. There is a complete inability to recover the account once the grace period officially terminates.

## Are product reviews or public Q&A posts deleted with my account?

Product reviews and public Q&A posts undergo anonymization or removal per customer request during the deletion process. Northvale strips personal identifiers, such as display names and profile links, from any publicly visible community contributions. While the textual review content may remain to preserve product feedback integrity, authorship attribution is permanently severed. Customers must specify review handling preferences through support if they require complete removal of public commentary. This approach balances community review transparency with individual privacy rights under Northvale policy.
