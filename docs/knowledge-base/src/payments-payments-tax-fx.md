# Northvale Payments Tax and Foreign Exchange Policy
## How is US state and local sales tax calculated at checkout?

US state and local sales tax is calculated at checkout based strictly on the destination shipping address provided by customers. Northvale applies destination-based tax calculation rules automatically to every transaction. State tax applies to both physical items and taxable shipping charges where required by local legislation. The exact tax percentage is computed dynamically before final order submission. Customers can review the itemized tax breakdown on the final payment screen.

## Does Northvale collect VAT or GST on international orders?

Northvale collects regional VAT or GST directly at checkout for eligible international orders to streamline customs processing. For other destinations, regional value-added taxes are collected separately by customs authorities upon arrival. Northvale ensures all collected taxes and duties are clearly itemized on the commercial invoice provided with the shipment. International customers should review local tax laws to understand whether checkout collection applies. Regional tax rates are determined automatically by the destination country.

## Are shipping charges subject to sales tax?

Shipping charges are subject to sales tax depending entirely on state-by-state taxability rules and statutory regulations. Northvale evaluates delivery fee taxability based on the specific destination jurisdiction of each order. For global shipments, the international shipping base fee of $24.95 is subject to applicable international tax treatments. Customers can view exact shipping tax amounts during the final checkout review stage before payment submission. Local tax authorities dictate whether freight and handling fees incur state sales tax.

## How can tax-exempt organizations register a tax-exemption certificate?

Tax-exempt organizations can register a valid tax-exemption certificate by uploading the documentation directly through the account settings portal before checkout. Northvale requires this verification review to be completed successfully before any tax-free ordering can take place. The uploaded certificate undergoes automated and manual compliance checks by the payments team. Once verified, the account status updates to reflect tax-exempt purchasing privileges. Customers must ensure their documentation remains valid and current to maintain tax-free status.

## Can I request a sales tax refund if I forgot to apply my exemption?

Customers can request a post-purchase sales tax adjustment by submitting a valid exemption certificate to customer support after the order is placed. Northvale issues a tax-only credit back to the original payment method once the certificate is verified. The exemption certificate must cover the exact date on which the purchase transaction occurred. Requests must be submitted in accordance with standard financial processing timelines. Customer support verifies the documentation before processing any tax-exclusive adjustments.

## Which currencies are supported for international checkout?

Northvale supports a wide range of international display and settlement currencies for global customers during checkout. The platform utilizes automatic geo-currency detection based on the user location while maintaining a manual currency switcher. Customers can select their preferred currency from the available dropdown menu before completing the payment process. All supported currencies reflect current market valuations maintained by financial partners. Transaction totals update immediately upon selecting a new display currency.

## How are multi-currency exchange rates determined?

Multi-currency exchange rates are determined by applying a daily mid-market reference rate that locks in at the exact moment of checkout. Northvale secures this exchange rate so that the authorized total remains stable during payment processing. The rate is fixed permanently once the financial institution authorizes the order transaction. Customers are protected against intraday currency fluctuations once the checkout rate is locked. This mechanism ensures transparency for all international transactions processed on the platform.

## What foreign transaction fees might my card issuer charge?

Card issuers typically charge a foreign currency conversion fee ranging from 1% to 3% for international transactions. Northvale does not assess, collect, or benefit from these card-issuer cross-border fees. Customers should consult their specific bank agreement or credit card terms to understand potential issuer charges. These fees appear separately on the bank statement issued by the card provider. The stated percentage depends entirely on the policies of the issuing financial institution.

## How does currency conversion affect international wire transfers?

International wire transfers are subject to a wire transfer minimum order threshold of $5,000 and a standard wire transfer fee of $25. Northvale advises customers to send funds in United States Dollars to avoid intermediary bank conversion short-pays. Sending alternative currencies through wire transfers can introduce third-party bank exchange fees and processing delays. The wire transfer fee covers standard banking processing costs incurred during manual reconciliation. Customers must ensure exact transfer amounts include all applicable wire fees.

## Why can the final tax differ slightly from the initial authorization?

The final tax can differ slightly from the initial authorization due to the maximum authorization buffer percentage of 20% applied for variable calculations. Northvale utilizes this authorized buffer to account for complex multi-jurisdictional tax adjustments before settlement. The final receipt reflects the exact statutory tax mandated by local governing authorities upon capture. Any temporary authorization hold differences are released automatically by the issuing bank according to standard banking cycles. Customers always pay the exact statutory tax amount calculated at settlement.

## How are taxes refunded on partial returns or cancelled items?

Taxes are refunded on a proportional basis when line items are returned or cancelled from an order. Northvale calculates the exact sales tax refund using the original tax rate applied to the specific returned items. Non-refundable fees associated with the transaction retain their applicable tax rules without modification. The proportional tax credit is returned directly to the original payment method used for the purchase. Refund processing times depend on the financial institution handling the transaction.

## Are digital receipts and VAT invoices available for business accounting?

Digital receipts and downloadable PDF tax invoices are readily available in the customer order history for business accounting purposes. Northvale includes the merchant tax identification number and a detailed itemized tax breakdown on every invoice. Business customers can access and download these financial documents at any time following order fulfillment. The generated invoices comply with standard record-keeping requirements for tax reporting. Customers can retrieve past invoices by logging into their account dashboard.

## How are duties vs sales taxes separated on international receipts?

Import customs duties are clearly distinguished from local VAT and GST on all international order receipts. Northvale separates these charges into distinct line items on the checkout summary and final commercial invoice. Customs duties represent fees levied by border authorities for importing goods, whereas sales taxes apply to regional consumption. Customers can view the precise breakdown of taxes and duties before confirming the purchase. This itemization ensures complete transparency regarding cross-border import costs.

## What if my tax-exempt status expires mid-year?

Tax-exempt organizations receive automated annual certificate renewal reminders prior to the expiration date of their documentation. Northvale applies standard sales tax automatically once a tax exemption certificate expires and has not been renewed. Organizations must upload updated compliance documents to prevent the assessment of standard sales taxes on future orders. Expired certificates cannot be applied retroactively to pending purchases. Maintaining active documentation ensures uninterrupted tax-free purchasing privileges.

## Why does my bank statement show a slightly different amount in local currency?

Bank statements may display a slightly different local currency amount due to the card-issuer foreign exchange spread and a conversion fee range of 1% to 3%. Northvale charges the exact checkout amount, but card issuers apply their own conversion rates during settlement. The difference arises from the variance between the checkout exchange rate and the issuer settlement rate. Customers should review their cardholder agreement for specific details regarding issuer-side conversion pricing. These pricing discrepancies are managed entirely by the card-issuing bank.
