# Legal Holds and Statutory Retention Policy
## Why can an account deletion request be blocked or delayed by open orders?

An account deletion request can be blocked or delayed by open orders because active shipments, pending returns, or open refunds require transaction finalization. Northvale must process all active commercial activities before an account profile can be fully erased. Customers must complete or cancel all open transactions before submitting a new deletion request. The system automatically halts the deletion workflow when active financial liabilities or pending logistics are detected. This safeguard ensures that all merchant obligations and customer deliveries are properly fulfilled.

## How do pending payment disputes or chargebacks affect deletion?

Pending payment disputes or chargebacks affect deletion by triggering a mandatory hold while the billing dispute is active. Northvale cannot erase customer profile data while financial investigations or chargeback claims remain unresolved. Customers may re-submit their account deletion request once the active dispute officially resolves. The deletion workflow stays suspended until financial institutions settle the outstanding inquiry. This mandatory hold protects both parties during ongoing financial reviews and investigations.

## Which records must Northvale retain even after account deletion?

Northvale must retain specific financial and statutory records even after account profile erasure is completed. Profile personally identifiable information is permanently erased, while statutory ledger records are preserved under legal obligations. Tax laws and regulatory audit requirements mandate the retention of specific transaction documents. These exempt records are isolated and retained strictly for compliance purposes. Customers receive a deletion summary that clarifies which transaction data remains preserved.

## How long are itemized order and tax records retained by law?

Itemized order and tax records are retained by law for a period of 7 years following the transaction date. Northvale maintains these financial ledgers to satisfy federal tax compliance mandates and statutory audit requirements. This mandatory retention schedule applies to all commercial invoices and payment histories. The system automatically secures these ledgers away from erased profile data. Regulatory frameworks strictly require this duration of record preservation for commercial entities.

## How long are security and fraud-prevention logs retained after deletion?

Security and fraud-prevention logs are retained for a period of 5 years after account deletion. Northvale preserves these security records to ensure network protection and ongoing abuse prevention. These logs maintain critical threat intelligence and historical access patterns for fraud detection. The data remains strictly isolated for security analysis and auditability. This retention period supports platform integrity and protects against malicious actors.

## How long are customer support transcripts retained after account erasure?

Customer support transcripts are retained for a period of 3 years after account erasure. Northvale maintains these communication histories to satisfy internal audit standards and quality-assurance compliance. Support records assist in resolving post-deletion inquiries and verifying previous service interactions. These archives are securely stored in restricted compliance environments. Once the required timeframe elapses, support records are permanently purged from all systems.

## How long does marketing telemetry remain before purge?

Marketing telemetry remains for a period of 2 years before being permanently purged from analytical systems. Northvale applies an immediate marketing opt-out upon deletion while retaining analytical logs for compliance reporting. This operational telemetry measures historical campaign performance without linking to deleted identities. The data is completely scrubbed from active targeting systems immediately upon request. All associated telemetry is automatically deleted after the required retention window.

## How are retained ledger records decoupled from my deleted profile?

Retained ledger records are decoupled from the deleted profile by stripping non-essential profile attributes while keeping statutory invoice fields. Northvale isolates mandatory tax and order documentation from personal identifiers. Access to these preserved financial records is strictly restricted to compliance-only roles. This architectural separation ensures that transactional compliance is maintained without preserving the user profile. The remaining ledger data contains no active marketing or behavioral attributes.

## What happens during the grace period if a new dispute is filed?

An automatic pause of the data purge occurs if a new dispute arrives mid-grace-period. Northvale freezes the deletion workflow to preserve necessary defense evidence for the incoming chargeback. Customers cannot bypass legal obligations by initiating deletion while new claims emerge. The system extends the retention hold until the newly filed dispute achieves complete resolution. This mechanism guarantees that all transactional evidence remains accessible for legal defense.

## How fast are non-exempt operational records purged after the grace period?

Non-exempt operational records are purged within a data deletion purge SLA of 48 hours after the data deletion grace period of 30 days concludes. Northvale executes this automated clean-up across all active databases and operational storage layers. There is a strict split between erasable profile data and exempt statutory ledgers during this phase. The operational purge removes all non-exempt user attributes, preferences, and personal files. This rapid execution ensures timely compliance with privacy regulations.

## When are exempt records cleared from encrypted backup snapshots?

Exempt records are cleared from encrypted backup snapshots according to a backup archive purge cycle of 30 days for non-exempt data. Northvale cycles all encrypted backup archives to ensure historical operational data is systematically overwritten. Statutory data that falls under legal holds remains securely stored only in the dedicated compliance archive. Backup snapshots naturally expire and rotate out of retention pools according to the standard schedule. This process aligns disaster recovery archives with ongoing data governance mandates.

## Can a banned or suspended fraud account use deletion to evade a ban?

A banned or suspended fraud account cannot use deletion to evade a ban because security and fraud retention keeps abuse-prevention signals intact. Northvale prohibits ban evasion via delete-and-reregister tactics by preserving essential risk identifiers. The platform maintains necessary device and account fingerprints within security isolation databases. These preserved signals prevent suspended actors from creating new unauthorized profiles. Account deletion removes consumer profile data but does not erase historical fraud enforcement markers.

## Do warranty claims still work if I deleted my account?

Warranty claims still work if the account is deleted, provided the customer retains the original PDF invoice for the manufacturer warranty. Northvale removes self-service portal access to order histories once profile erasure is finalized. Customers must utilize their downloaded offline invoice documentation to substantiate warranty claims directly with manufacturers. The loss of portal access prevents retrieval of order histories through Northvale systems after deletion. Retaining offline documentation is necessary for managing third-party warranties post-deletion.

## How will I know which data was deleted vs retained under legal hold?

Customers know which data was deleted versus retained under legal hold through a comprehensive deletion confirmation summary distinguishing purged records from statutory records. Northvale provides this detailed post-deletion report to verify compliance actions. The summary outlines all personal data categories that were permanently erased from the system. Customers may contact the privacy team for further clarification regarding specific retention categories. This transparency ensures full visibility into the execution of the deletion request.

## Are retained tax records ever used for marketing or profiling?

Retained tax records are strictly prohibited from being used for marketing, profiling, or any non-compliance purpose. Northvale enforces rigid access-control isolation to ensure held records remain untouched by commercial systems. These statutory files are locked away exclusively for regulatory and tax audit functions. Commercial teams have no operational access to data preserved under legal holds. This strict limitation protects consumer privacy while satisfying mandatory legal retention duties.
