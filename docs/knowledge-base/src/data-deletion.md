# Account Data Deletion FAQ

## How do I request the deletion of my account?

You can submit an official request to delete your Northvale account directly through your online account settings page or by contacting customer support. To ensure account security and prevent unauthorized erasure, submitting a deletion request requires completing identity verification using your account password and a one-time verification code (OTP) sent to your registered email. Once your deletion request is verified, a formal confirmation notice is emailed to your address within 24 hours. Your account immediately enters a mandatory 30-day grace period before permanent erasure is executed. If you change your mind during this window, cancelling the deletion request restores your account to full standing with all settings preserved.

## What personal information is permanently deleted?

When your account deletion request executes following the grace period, our systems permanently remove your account profile, login credentials, and all saved shipping addresses. All stored payment methods, including tokenized credit card profiles and billing configurations, are completely expunged from our payment processor vault. Your customer-facing order history is removed from active platform databases, except for specific records that Northvale is legally obligated to maintain. In addition, customer support conversation histories, email communications, and support tickets are permanently purged. All recorded marketing communication preferences and advertising analytics tracking identifiers are completely deleted.

## What data is retained after account deletion?

In accordance with legal and statutory requirements, itemized order records and associated payment transaction logs are retained for 7 years to ensure compliance with federal tax and auditing regulations. Security records, fraud-prevention flags, and unauthorized activity logs are retained for 5 years to safeguard payment networks and prevent repeat abuse. Any account data subject to an active legal hold or pending judicial proceeding is retained for the duration required by court orders. Aggregated and fully anonymized platform analytics are retained indefinitely since they cannot be linked back to individual users. All retained archival data is strictly isolated in secure storage and is never utilized for commercial marketing.

## What is the timeline for data deletion?

The account deletion process begins with a mandatory 30-day grace period during which your account remains inactive but fully recoverable. Once the 30-day grace period officially concludes, permanent database deletion executes across all operational systems within 48 hours. Secure system backup archives that contain snapshot data are cycled and permanently purged within 30 days of production deletion execution. Any archival data subject to statutory compliance retention outlined in Section 3 persists securely for its specifically mandated statutory retention period. Following successful execution of the deletion script, users may receive a final automated email confirming that their account data has been erased.

## Can I recover a deleted account or create a new one?

You can recover and reactivate your deleted account exclusively while the account remains within the initial 30-day grace period window. Recovering an account during this grace period requires successfully authenticating with your original account email address and verified password. Once the 30-day grace period has elapsed and permanent deletion executes, your account cannot be restored or recovered under any circumstances. You are permitted to register an entirely new Northvale account in the future using the same email address, but no historical orders, loyalty credits, or preferences will be linked to the new profile. Saved payment methods are permanently removed and must be manually re-entered.
