# Order Cancellation Policy

## Cancellation Window

Customers are entitled to free cancellation on standard orders up until the order fulfillment status officially transitions to "Shipped". Once an order is marked as shipped, cancellation is strictly disallowed; customers must instead follow the standard return process after delivery. In addition, orders cannot be cancelled while displaying an active "Processing" status if more than 24 hours have elapsed since initial order placement. Under normal warehouse operations, the standard window elapsed between initial processing and final shipping spans 24 to 48 hours. Orders submitted over the weekend may experience accelerated processing schedules; customers should review real-time order status before assuming cancelability.

## Pre-Order Cancellation

Customers placing advance pre-orders retain the flexibility to cancel their pre-ordered items up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled official product release date. Any cancellation requests submitted within 48 hours of the scheduled release date are strictly not permitted. When a pre-order cancellation is successfully processed within the eligible window, all associated pre-order deposits are refunded in full to the customer. For multi-item orders containing both standard inventory and pre-ordered goods, the order is cancelled as a single unit unless separate fulfillment split options were selected at checkout. If Northvale announces a revised official release date, the cancellation window automatically extends to the new date.

## Bulk Order Cancellation

Purchases categorized as bulk orders comprising 10 or more units require formal written notice submitted at least 24 hours prior to cancellation. If a bulk order involves custom-manufactured or made-to-order goods and cancellation occurs after production has started, the transaction incurs a 15% cancellation fee. Conversely, bulk cancellation requests submitted before manufacturing or customization production begins are refunded in full. When a customer executes a partial cancellation of an existing bulk order, Northvale reserves the right to reprice all remaining items according to single-unit retail rates. Tiered bulk pricing discounts no longer apply to orders that fall below qualifying thresholds following partial cancellations.

## Cancellation After Shipping

Orders that have completed dispatch and entered shipped status cannot be cancelled under any circumstances; the recipient must initiate a standard return within 30 days. For the fastest operational resolution, customers wishing to cancel a package already in transit may choose to refuse delivery at the door. Refused packages are automatically redirected and returned to the Northvale fulfillment facility at no additional shipping cost to the customer. All associated monetary refunds are processed only after the returned package is received back and inspected at the intake dock. Standard return shipping fees are waived for refused parcel deliveries returned through carrier transit.

## Refund on Cancellation

When an order is successfully cancelled within eligible policy rules, all refunded balances are issued directly to the customer's original payment method. The electronic refund transaction is initiated within 2 hours of confirmed cancellation by our automated billing systems. For transactions originally charged to debit cards, financial institutions may require up to 10 business days for the funds to appear on account statements. All outbound shipping charges are refunded in full for orders successfully cancelled prior to warehouse dispatch. However, shipping and postage fees are non-refundable for order cancellation attempts submitted after warehouse dispatch has already occurred.
