# Exchange FAQ

## What items are eligible for an exchange?

You can request an exchange on eligible items within 30 days of carrier delivery confirmation, following the identical timeframe established for standard returns. To qualify for an exchange, your item must satisfy all return condition requirements, arriving unworn, unwashed, and with all original manufacturer tags attached. Exchanges are strictly limited to direct size and color variations of the exact same product originally ordered. Products designated as final sale, custom-made merchandise, or personalized goods cannot be exchanged under any circumstances. You are permitted one exchange per order line; any subsequent changes require completing a full return followed by a brand-new purchase.

## How do size and color exchanges work with inventory?

Size exchanges are permitted for any corresponding size option currently in stock at the time your exchange request is submitted. Color exchanges are strictly limited to the color variants that were commercially available at the time of your original purchase. In the event that your desired size or color is completely sold out, an exchange cannot be fulfilled; our support team will issue a refund or store credit instead. To protect your selection, requested exchange inventory is reserved and held for 48 hours after your request is formally submitted. Please note that discounted sale items can only be exchanged at their original sale purchase price.

## What is the step-by-step exchange process?

You can initiate a product exchange online through your Northvale account portal or by contacting our customer support team directly. Once submitted, our system generates a prepaid shipping label for you to affix to the return parcel for the return transit leg. A replacement shipment is dispatched automatically as soon as the returned merchandise receives its initial carrier intake scan. If you require urgent delivery, expedited replacement shipping can be selected for an optional $12.95 flat fee. Under standard fulfillment, replacement orders are dispatched within 2 to 3 business days following verified carrier scanning.

## Who pays for exchange shipping and surcharges?

The return transit leg is provided free of charge whenever you are exchanging an item that arrived defective or was shipped incorrectly due to our fulfillment error. For standard change-of-mind exchanges, the customer is responsible for a $6.95 return shipping postage fee. Replacement outbound shipping is completely free for all qualifying US orders totaling over $50. For all international exchanges, the customer is responsible for paying return shipping costs in both directions. In addition, exchange shipments delivering to addresses in Alaska and Hawaii remain subject to the standard $12.00 regional shipping surcharge.

## How are pricing differences and discounts calculated?

If you exchange an item for a higher-priced variation, you are required to pay the price difference during online checkout before the exchange is finalized. If you select a lower-priced variation, the calculated difference is refunded directly to your original payment method. Northvale does not apply price protection adjustments to exchanges; all exchange pricing strictly matches the current live catalog listing. When exchanging an item previously purchased on sale for a regular-priced item, you will be charged the current regular price difference. Furthermore, previous limited-time promotional discounts do not carry over to newly exchanged items.
