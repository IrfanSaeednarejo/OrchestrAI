# SMS Notifications Policy and FAQ
## What types of transactional SMS messages does Northvale send?

Northvale sends transactional SMS messages strictly for account security OTPs, security alerts, and urgent delivery exceptions. Transactional SMS differs completely from promotional SMS, which covers marketing and sales offers. Customers receive these vital alerts directly to their registered mobile devices. These operational notices ensure continuous visibility into account access and shipment statuses. Northvale maintains this operational separation to protect user privacy and account security.

## How do I opt in to receive transactional SMS notifications?

Customers opt in to receive transactional SMS notifications by entering and verifying their mobile number in Notification Settings. This process requires confirming the explicit SMS consent checkbox during the setup procedure. Northvale validates the mobile device before enabling any operational text messaging. The active verification step secures the notification channel against unauthorized access. Users must complete both actions to activate transactional text alerts.

## Why is promotional SMS opt-in separate from transactional SMS?

Promotional SMS opt-in remains separate from transactional SMS to comply with dual-consent separation rules mandated by the TCPA and carriers. Northvale enforces this distinct consent framework for all registered account profiles. Opting out of marketing SMS does not block security OTPs or essential delivery updates. Customers retain independent control over promotional and transactional messaging preferences. This structural division ensures critical account security notices are never restricted by marketing choices.

## How quickly are order dispatch SMS and email alerts sent?

Order dispatch SMS and email alerts are sent according to a specific notification dispatch delay SLA of 4 hours. The tracking activation SLA is also maintained at 4 hours following carrier acceptance. Northvale utilizes real-time carrier sync to process shipment data efficiently. Customers receive timely updates as soon as the dispatch events are logged. This synchronization guarantees reliable tracking visibility for all outbound packages.

## How fast are delivery exception SMS alerts triggered?

Delivery exception SMS alerts are triggered within a transit exception alert delay SLA of 2 hours. Northvale systems monitor carrier scans continuously to detect any unexpected transit delays or failed delivery attempts. When an exception occurs, automated triggers dispatch notifications to the customer mobile device immediately. This rapid alerting mechanism keeps recipients informed of route obstructions without delay. Customers can review shipment statuses instantly upon receiving these exception notices.

## How long are SMS verification OTPs valid?

SMS verification OTPs remain valid for an exact duration of 10 minutes. Customers must complete the verification process within this designated window before the code expires. Northvale advises keeping the mobile phone signal active during the verification procedure to prevent delivery delays. If the code expires, users can request a new verification token through the secure login portal. Prompt entry ensures successful authentication and immediate access to account features.

## How do I stop receiving SMS messages using carrier keywords?

Customers stop receiving SMS messages by replying with the keyword STOP to opt out of the SMS channel completely. Northvale processes this carrier keyword instantly to halt further outgoing text transmissions. The complementary keyword HELP returns essential support contact information to the mobile device. Using these standard carrier keywords provides an effortless method for managing communication channels. The automated system recognizes these commands across all supported mobile networks.

## How quickly does an SMS opt-out take effect?

An SMS opt-out takes effect operationally within an unsubscribe effect window of 24 hours. Northvale applies immediate automated suppression for all incoming STOP replies sent by customers. The full system-wide synchronization across carrier gateways completes inside the stated timeframe. During this operational window, all queued transactional text alerts cease. Customers can verify their unsubscribed status directly through their account notification preferences.

## Can I re-subscribe to SMS alerts after texting STOP?

Customers can re-subscribe to SMS alerts by texting START or by re-enabling the preference within Notification Settings. Northvale requires a fresh phone verification step if the registered mobile number has changed. The reactivation process restores transactional text alerts following successful validation. Customers retain the flexibility to resume updates at any time through standard account interfaces. Immediate notification delivery resumes once the updated consent is recorded.

## Are message and data rates charged by my mobile carrier?

Northvale sends all transactional SMS messages completely free of charge, though standard carrier message and data rates may apply. Customers should consult their individual mobile service agreements for specific billing details. International SMS carrier support depends on participating global network providers and local roaming agreements. Northvale absorbs no fees related to customer mobile carrier data usage. Users remain responsible for any charges assessed by their respective telecommunication providers.

## What happens if I change my mobile phone number?

Changing a mobile phone number requires updating and re-verifying the new contact detail within account settings. Northvale executes an automatic unlinking process to prevent sensitive alerts from going to recycled numbers. This protective measure safeguards personal data against unauthorized interception by subsequent phone owners. Customers must complete the verification process for the new device before notifications resume. Maintaining current phone details ensures uninterrupted delivery of security OTPs.

## Why am I not receiving SMS OTPs on my phone?

Missing SMS OTPs typically stem from carrier short-code blocking, active Do Not Disturb settings, or temporary roaming delays. Northvale recommends checking device configurations and contacting the mobile provider if issues persist. As a reliable alternative, customers can select email OTP fallback options during the login sequence. This alternative pathway ensures account access remains available even when mobile networks experience delivery disruptions. System diagnostics can also verify whether carrier networks are rejecting short-code messages.

## Are security login alerts sent via SMS or email?

Security login alerts default to email notifications while offering optional SMS delivery as an added layer. Northvale ensures that critical credential changes always trigger mandatory notifications across primary channels. Customers can customize their alert preferences within the account security dashboard. This dual-channel approach balances convenience with robust protection against unauthorized account modifications. Important security notices are prioritized to maintain absolute account integrity.

## Does Northvale share my mobile number with third-party marketers?

Northvale strictly prohibits selling or sharing SMS opt-in numbers with third-party marketers or external commercial entities. Mobile numbers collected for transactional notifications are maintained under strict privacy guidelines. Transmission occurs exclusively through secure carrier gateways required for message delivery. Customer data remains protected against unauthorized commercial exploitation or secondary distribution. Trust and privacy represent foundational priorities across all operational notification workflows.

## How do I update my SMS preferences without deleting my account?

Updating SMS preferences without deleting an account is accomplished by navigating to Account Settings, selecting Notifications, and opening SMS Preferences. Northvale provides granular toggles allowing users to manage specific notification types independently. Customers can adjust their alert settings at any time without impacting their overall account status. The interface enables precise control over transactional and operational message delivery channels. Saving these configuration changes updates the notification system immediately.
