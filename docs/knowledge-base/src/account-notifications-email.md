# Northvale Email Notification Policy
## What types of emails does Northvale send to customers?

Northvale sends both mandatory transactional emails and optional marketing digests to registered customers. Transactional messages include purchase receipts, shipping updates, and crucial account security alerts. Optional communications feature regular newsletters and promotional digests about store offerings. Customers can easily manage their mailing preferences through the account settings portal. Maintaining this balance ensures transparency while delivering essential operational notifications.

## Can I opt out of transactional order and security emails?

Customers cannot opt out of mandatory transactional order and security emails under any circumstances. Receipts, password reset confirmations, and critical security alerts remain necessary for account safety. Legal compliance and operational verification dictate that these communications cannot be disabled. Northvale requires active delivery channels for these notices to protect user data. Every registered account must maintain valid communication pathways for essential security updates.

## How do I unsubscribe from promotional marketing emails and digests?

Customers can unsubscribe from promotional marketing emails and digests using the one-click footer link in any campaign message. Alternatively, individuals may toggle their preferences directly within the Notification Settings menu of their account. The footer unsubscribe link requires no login credentials for immediate convenience. Processing this request removes the address from future promotional campaign distributions. Automated systems promptly update the mailing lists following each opt-out submission.

## How quickly does an email unsubscribe take effect?

An email unsubscribe takes effect operationally within 24 hours across all systems. The maximum legal processing window complies with CAN-SPAM regulations, requiring completion within 10 business days. In-flight queued campaigns already dispatched prior to the request may arrive briefly. Customers should disregard any promotional materials sent during this brief transition window. System synchronization ensures complete removal of the address from active marketing databases.

## How do back-in-stock and price-drop email alerts work?

Back-in-stock and price-drop email alerts function through per-product alert signups located on specific out-of-stock item pages. Customers register their interest to receive automated notifications when inventory levels replenish. The system triggers a single automatic message when the product becomes available again. Once sent, the individual alert registration automatically expires to prevent duplicate messaging. Users must submit a new request if they wish to monitor future inventory cycles.

## How can I configure email digest frequency or quiet hours?

Customers can configure email digest frequency by toggling between weekly summaries and real-time promotional updates in Notification Settings. The platform also applies quiet-hours delivery windowing for non-urgent promotional messages to respect local time zones. Adjusting these parameters tailors the timing of commercial arrivals to personal preferences. Real-time updates dispatch immediately, whereas weekly digests bundle communications into a single periodic message. Proper configuration reduces inbox clutter while maintaining access to relevant store announcements.

## How quickly are order dispatch and transit exception emails sent?

Order dispatch notifications adhere to a strict fulfillment SLA of 4 hours following package pickup. Transit exception alerts generate within 2 hours of receiving a carrier delay signal. Each notification includes a direct carrier tracking link for real-time shipment monitoring. Automated systems pull carrier telemetry to ensure timely and accurate delivery reporting. Customers receive immediate updates regarding any unexpected routing changes during transit.

## How quickly are account deletion confirmation emails sent?

Account deletion confirmation emails are dispatched within a strict SLA of 24 hours following a closure request. This message verifies that personal data removal procedures have initiated successfully across the platform. Customers must check their spam or junk folders if the confirmation notice does not arrive promptly. Support channels remain available to assist individuals who fail to receive the verification receipt. Immediate confirmation provides assurance that the account closure workflow is progressing properly.

## What happens if my email address bounces repeatedly?

A repeatedly bouncing email address triggers permanent hard-bounce suppression or a temporary soft bounce status within the system. The platform distinguishes between transient delivery failures and permanent mailbox rejections to protect sender reputation. Accounts experiencing delivery issues display a dashboard warning banner advising users to update their email address. Suppressed addresses stop receiving all non-transactional messages until the delivery error resolves. Promptly updating invalid contact details prevents missed operational and security notifications.

## How do I remove my email from a bounce suppression list?

Removing an email from a bounce suppression list requires verifying mailbox capacity and contacting support to clear hard-bounce flags. Customers can also update their profile to an active email address authenticated via an OTP verification code. Support agents manually review cleared suppression records to restore standard message delivery. Maintaining adequate inbox storage prevents recurring bounce suppressions from activating. Restoring proper deliverability ensures uninterrupted receipt of important account communications.

## Why are Northvale emails landing in my Spam or Promotions tab?

Northvale emails may land in spam or promotions tabs due to aggressive inbound email filter rules applied by mailbox providers. Customers can prevent this by adding the official sender domain to their contacts or whitelist. The underlying infrastructure fully enforces SPF, DKIM, and DMARC authentication protocols to verify message legitimacy. Mailbox providers occasionally misclassify automated commercial content despite valid security signatures. Users should mark legitimate messages as not spam to train their personal inbox filters effectively.

## What happens to my email preferences if I change my primary email?

Notification preferences seamlessly migrate to the new verified email address whenever a customer updates their primary contact. Security alert notifications are concurrently sent to both the old and new addresses for verification purposes. This dual-notification protocol protects against unauthorized account modifications and fraudulent address changes. The legacy email address loses all association with the account once the migration completes. Updated preferences immediately apply to all future communications sent to the new destination.

## How do I re-subscribe to marketing digests after unsubscribing?

Customers can re-subscribe to marketing digests by re-enabling promotional emails within their Account Settings page. This action requires explicit opt-in consent to satisfy commercial messaging regulations. The system immediately updates the distribution database to include the reactivated address in future campaigns. Past preferences or customized categories may need reconfiguration following the re-subscription. Promotional messages resume according to the newly saved schedule and frequency settings.

## Does unsubscribing from marketing emails delete my account?

Unsubscribing from marketing emails leaves the core account, order history, and store credits completely intact. The action exclusively disables the promotional mailing list flag associated with the user profile. Customers retain full access to their dashboard, saved addresses, and active orders. Marketing opt-outs strictly govern commercial communications and have no effect on account status. The platform continues to process transactional notices regardless of promotional subscription choices.

## How do I report a suspicious phishing email impersonating Northvale?

Suspicious phishing emails impersonating Northvale should be reported by forwarding the message directly to security support without clicking any embedded links. Northvale never asks for passwords, full credit card numbers, or sensitive authentication details via email. Reporting fraudulent messages helps security teams identify threats and protect the broader customer community. Automated verification tools analyze forwarded samples to trace malicious sender infrastructure. Vigilance against phishing attempts safeguards personal information from unauthorized access.
