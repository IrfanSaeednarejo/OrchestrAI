# Instant Swap Orders and Exchanges Policy
## How does an instant advance exchange work?

An instant advance exchange dispatches a replacement item immediately upon receiving a carrier drop-off scan or placing a temporary card hold. This process differs from a standard return-then-ship method by shipping the new item before the original product arrives back at the warehouse. Customers initiate this streamlined procedure through the online portal to secure their preferred replacement. Northvale designed this service to minimize the waiting period typically associated with standard mail-in exchanges. The temporary card authorization acts as a security measure until the initial merchandise reaches the facility.

## Which items qualify for a size or variant exchange?

Items qualify for a size or variant exchange within the standard exchange window of 30 days from delivery confirmation. Northvale requires that the requested item belongs to the same-product SKU family and remains currently in stock. Customers must select eligible apparel or variant items directly through the automated returns portal. This policy ensures seamless processing for standard inventory modifications without manual intervention. Additional restrictions apply to promotional items and cross-product exchanges as outlined in the general terms.

## How long is exchange inventory reserved after I submit a request?

Exchange inventory is reserved for exactly 48 hours after a customer submits an online exchange request. Northvale automatically releases the held stock back into general inventory if the return label is not scanned within that timeframe. Customers must complete the carrier drop-off within this designated window to maintain their product reservation. The system notifies users when the hold period approaches expiration to prevent automatic cancellation. Securing replacement stock promptly guarantees availability during high-demand shopping periods.

## Why is a temporary card authorization placed for instant exchanges?

A temporary card authorization is placed for instant exchanges to cover the replacement value until the original item returns. Northvale uses this temporary hold to guarantee the safe return of the initial merchandise. The authorization does not constitute an immediate charge on the customer account. This pending hold drops automatically upon warehouse receipt and inspection of the returned parcel. Financial institutions typically release the pending funds in accordance with their standard processing schedules.

## What happens if the original item is not returned on time?

The temporary card hold converts into a captured charge if the original item is not returned on time. Northvale sends automated reminder notifications before executing any charge capture on the customer payment method. Customers must ship the merchandise back promptly to avoid paying for both the original and replacement items. Once the captured charge occurs, the transaction becomes final and the merchandise remains with the customer. Support teams monitor these deadlines strictly to maintain inventory accuracy across all fulfillment centers.

## How are price differences handled when swapping variants?

Price differences are handled by processing an even swap for same-price size and color variants. Northvale applies a refund or an incremental charge whenever customers swap for differently priced variants within the same SKU family. The payment system automatically calculates any balance adjustments at the time of the online exchange submission. Customers approve any additional charges prior to finalizing the replacement order creation. Price adjustments reflect the current catalog pricing of the newly selected variant.

## How fast is an exchange replacement order dispatched?

An exchange replacement order is dispatched within 2 to 3 business days after the initial carrier drop-off scan. Northvale prioritizes these replacement shipments to ensure timely delivery to the customer. Customers can also select an expedited replacement shipping option for an additional fee during checkout. Tracking information updates automatically as soon as the fulfillment center processes the outbound package. This dispatch timeline applies to all standard domestic exchange requests processed through the portal.

## How much does return or expedited shipping cost for an exchange?

Change-of-mind return postage incurs a fee of 6.95 dollars for the first item, while an expedited replacement shipping option costs 12.95 dollars. Northvale provides completely free return shipping for defective or wrong items reported through the support system. Customers viewing the portal will see all applicable fees clearly itemized before confirming their request. The standard return postage fee is deducted from any applicable refund or charged during the exchange creation. Alaska and Hawaii destinations incur a regional surcharge for certain shipping methods.

## Is outbound replacement shipping free on instant exchanges?

Outbound replacement shipping is free on instant exchanges for qualifying orders meeting the standard minimum order threshold of 50 dollars. Northvale applies this free shipping benefit automatically to eligible customer accounts during the portal transaction. A regional surcharge of 12 dollars still applies to all shipments destined for Alaska and Hawaii. Customers must verify their delivery address to determine exact shipping qualifications before completing the exchange request. Standard delivery rules govern all outbound replacement transit times.

## Can I exchange an item bought with a promotional discount?

Customers can exchange an item bought with a promotional discount while preserving the original promotional discount on same-SKU size swaps. Northvale enforces a strict discount forfeiture policy on cross-product swaps where a different item is selected. The portal automatically calculates price adjustments based on whether the variant change qualifies for promotional preservation. Promotional pricing never carries over to items outside the original product family. Support representatives cannot manually override discount preservation rules for cross-product exchanges.

## Can I switch from an exchange to a refund before shipping the item back?

Customers can switch from an exchange to a refund before shipping the item back by cancelling the exchange request in the online portal. Northvale releases the inventory reservation immediately upon successful cancellation of the pending exchange. Customers must then submit a standard return request to process a monetary refund instead of a replacement. This action ensures the account correctly reflects the updated preference for a return. Once a replacement order ships, the transaction cannot be reversed into a standard refund.

## What if the requested exchange size sells out during transit?

The reservation hold protection secures the requested exchange size during the designated hold window. Northvale guarantees inventory allocation once the exchange request is successfully submitted and confirmed in the system. An automatic refund is offered if stock unexpectedly fails or becomes damaged in the fulfillment center. Customers receive prompt email notifications if an inventory discrepancy prevents the final replacement fulfillment. The system automatically updates the customer account status to process a refund in such rare instances.

## Can gift recipients request an instant size exchange?

Gift recipients can request an instant size exchange without notifying the original buyer through the online portal. Northvale requires valid gift order and receipt verification to process this type of transaction securely. The system validates the provided proof of purchase before issuing any replacement items or temporary holds. Gift exchanges follow the exact same operational rules and timelines as standard customer-initiated orders. Original buyers do not receive automated status updates regarding independent gift recipient exchange actions.

## How do I track both my returning parcel and replacement shipment?

Dual tracking links appear directly within the exchange portal to monitor both shipments simultaneously. Northvale configures the system so that the initial carrier drop-off scan officially triggers replacement dispatch. Customers can click the respective tracking numbers to view real-time transit progress for inbound and outbound parcels. Email updates also provide status milestones as packages move through the carrier network. This dual-tracking visibility ensures transparency throughout the entire instant exchange lifecycle.

## Can I exchange multiple items from one order in a single request?

Multi-item exchange cart selection in the portal allows customers to process several products in a single request. Northvale provides a consolidated return label to simplify shipping multiple items back in one package. Customers select each specific item and desired replacement variant before submitting the combined form. The system generates a single temporary card authorization covering the combined replacement value of all selected items. This consolidated approach reduces shipping waste and streamlines processing for the warehouse team.
