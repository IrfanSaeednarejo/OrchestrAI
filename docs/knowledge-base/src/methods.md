# Payment Methods FAQ

## What credit and debit cards can I use at Northvale?

Northvale accepts all major personal and business credit cards, including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. If you prefer to use a debit card, your card is seamlessly processed through the standard credit network; direct PIN-based debit transactions are not supported. To ensure transaction authorization, your card must be associated with a valid billing address located within the United States or an approved international destination. Customers may also utilize prepaid cards from Visa, Mastercard, or American Express, provided they are issued and verified by a supported card network. Please confirm your card has sufficient available credit or funds prior to completing checkout.

## Which digital wallets are supported for purchases?

You can complete your checkout quickly and securely using popular digital wallets including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. Completing transactions through a digital wallet requires maintaining a verified linked payment card or active checking bank account within the respective wallet provider. If an order purchased through a digital wallet is returned or refunded, all approved refund amounts are returned directly to the wallet balance rather than the underlying linked card. To safeguard against unauthorized transactions, digital wallet payments exceeding $500 may trigger an additional security verification challenge at checkout. Furthermore, any wallet-specific promotions or cash-back incentives apply automatically whenever available.

## How do Buy Now Pay Later installments work with Klarna?

Northvale partners with Klarna to offer flexible Buy Now Pay Later financing on qualifying customer orders valued between $50 and $2,000. Under this payment option, your order total is evenly split into four interest-free installment payments distributed over a 6-week timeframe. All credit eligibility, approvals, and transaction determinations are managed independently by Klarna rather than by Northvale. When you receive an approved refund on an order financed through Klarna, your remaining unpaid installments are cancelled, and previously settled installments are refunded within 5 business days. Importantly, experiencing late payments on a Klarna schedule does not negatively affect your account standing with Northvale.

## How do gift cards and store credit work at checkout?

Northvale offers gift cards issued in convenient digital formats delivered by email as well as traditional physical cards. Gift cards purchased from Northvale never expire and carry no dormancy or administrative maintenance fees over time. In addition, store credit may be issued to your account as compensation, return resolution, or through specialized promotional campaigns. During checkout, you can combine a digital gift card or accumulated store credit with one other accepted payment method to cover any remaining order balance. Please note that accumulated gift card balances and store credits are non-redeemable for cash, except in jurisdictions where cash redemption is strictly required by law.

## Can I pay directly using a bank transfer or wire?

Customers completing large purchases can utilize direct electronic automated clearing house (ACH) bank transfers on qualifying orders exceeding $200. Electronic ACH transfers require 3 business days to clear and settle before warehouse picking and parcel dispatch can commence. For substantial commercial purchases totaling over $5,000, Northvale accepts direct domestic wire transfers subject to an administrative wire processing fee of $25. In the event an ACH transfer fails due to insufficient funds (NSF) or account closure, the transaction incurs a $15 returned transfer fee. When orders paid via bank transfer are cancelled or refunded, processed funds take 3 business days to settle back to your account.

## How can I update or change my payment method?

You can modify or update the payment method assigned to an open order only while the order remains pending before warehouse dispatch occurs. Once an order dispatches, payment method modifications are no longer possible; resolving the order requires waiting for delivery, initiating a cancellation or return, and placing a new order. For recurring subscription services, you can update your stored payment method at any time through the billing dashboard. Any update made to your primary card on file applies automatically across all future subscription renewals and subsequent orders. Please note that simply deleting a saved card from your account wallet does not cancel active recurring subscriptions.
