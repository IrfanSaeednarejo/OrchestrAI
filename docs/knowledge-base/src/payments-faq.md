# Payment Terms and Billing FAQ

## What payment methods do you accept at checkout?

Northvale accepts major credit cards including Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover for online purchases. Debit cards carrying major card brand logos are fully processed through the standard credit network; direct PIN debit routing is not supported. You may also check out seamlessly using modern digital wallets including Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. For flexible installment financing, Buy Now Pay Later is available via Klarna on qualifying orders valued between $50 and $2,000. Additionally, digital or physical gift cards and accumulated store credit can be combined with one other accepted payment method on any transaction.

## How will charges appear on my billing statement?

All completed transactions appear clearly on your credit card or bank statements under the standardized descriptor "NORTHVALE*ORDER". This exact statement descriptor is also included in your official order confirmation email for immediate verification. If an order involves split shipments originating from different facilities, the transactions create separate distinct charges per shipment as items are dispatched. For recurring subscriptions, billing renewals occur on the same calendar day each month. If a subscription renewal charge fails, our billing system automatically retries the payment after 3, 5, and 7 days before initiating service cancellation.

## How do card authorizations and temporary holds work?

A temporary card authorization is placed on your account immediately at checkout and is formally captured when your order dispatches. These authorization holds expire automatically after 7 days if the order has not yet been dispatched from the warehouse. To account for variable regional carrier calculations, the initial authorization amount can exceed the final charge by up to 20% for estimated shipping. If you cancel an authorized order, debit card holds may take 3 to 5 business days to be released by your financial institution. Submitting multiple consecutive failed authorizations locks the payment card from further checkout retries for 24 hours.

## How are sales taxes, international duties, and currencies handled?

All product prices and shipping fees throughout the Northvale storefront are displayed in US Dollars (USD); foreign payment cards are converted directly by your card issuer. State and local sales taxes are calculated automatically based on your destination shipping address during final checkout. If your organization qualifies for tax exemption, you must upload your valid exemption certificate to the account portal prior to purchase. International orders are billed tax-exclusive at checkout, with all local import taxes and duties paid upon final delivery. Foreign currency conversion fees, typically ranging from 1% to 3%, are determined by your card issuer rather than Northvale.

## Why did my payment fail and how can I resolve it?

The most common causes of payment failure include an expired card, insufficient available funds, or an automated bank security decline. When a soft decline occurs, payment retry is allowed immediately; however, hard declines require a mandatory 24-hour waiting period before re-attempting. You will receive immediate failed-payment notifications via email and an account warning banner on your dashboard. Orders experiencing payment failure are held securely for 72 hours to allow payment resolution before auto-cancellation takes place. Incurring three consecutive failed transaction attempts will temporarily block your account from attempting checkout for 48 hours.

## How does Northvale protect my payment and card security?

Your complete payment card numbers are never stored on Northvale servers; all sensitive payment details are securely tokenized through our payment processor. Entering your card security code (CVV) is strictly required on every single transaction, and CVV values are never retained in storage. A 3-D Secure cardholder authentication challenge is automatically triggered for transactions exceeding $250 or originating from newly detected devices. All payment and billing data is encrypted and transmitted exclusively over TLS 1.3 protocol connections. Furthermore, Northvale's checkout infrastructure maintains full Level 1 compliance under the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS).
