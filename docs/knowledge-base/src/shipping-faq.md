# Shipping and Delivery FAQ

## What domestic shipping options can I choose?

You can choose from three domestic shipping tiers at checkout to suit your schedule and budget. Standard shipping delivers within 5 to 7 business days and is completely free on all qualifying domestic orders over $50. Expedited shipping delivers your package within 2 to 3 business days for a predictable $9.95 flat rate. For urgent needs, next-day shipping delivers within 1 business day for $19.95, provided your order is submitted by 12 pm Eastern Time. Please note that overnight and next-day delivery options are not available for deliveries directed to PO boxes or military APO and FPO addresses. Standard shipping is pre-selected as the default option during the checkout process.

## How long does order processing and dispatch take?

Standard order processing requires up to 24 hours to pick, verify, and pack your items before official dispatch. Any orders placed after 3 pm Eastern Time begin order fulfillment processing on the following business day. Orders placed over the weekend are prepared for dispatch on the following Monday morning. Once your parcel is dispatched from our distribution facility, carrier tracking numbers activate within 4 hours. All posted delivery date estimates reflect normal transit schedules and explicitly exclude weekend days, observed federal holidays, and severe weather delays encountered along carrier delivery routes.

## How can I track my shipment and receive updates?

Your tracking number is automatically emailed to your registered address immediately upon package dispatch. You can track real-time transit milestones directly from your Northvale online account portal at any hour. During checkout, you may also opt in to receive text message SMS notifications regarding shipment movements and delivery updates. If a transit exception occurs, delivery exception alerts are sent to your notifications within 2 hours of the carrier flag being posted. If a package cannot be delivered after two failed delivery attempts, the carrier will return the package to our warehouse after holding it for 7 days.

## Do you offer international delivery and customs clearance?

International shipping is currently available to 42 countries worldwide, and you can view the complete destination list on our dedicated international shipping page. International delivery typically takes 10 to 21 business days depending on your specific destination country and customs clearance processes. All applicable customs duties and import taxes assessed by local authorities remain the recipient's responsibility upon parcel arrival. For security compliance, international orders valued over $200 require a physical signature at delivery. Please be aware that Northvale does not ship packages to any jurisdictions currently subject to international trade embargoes.

## How are shipping costs, surcharges, and insurance calculated?

You receive free standard shipping on all US orders totaling over $50 before taxes and discounts are applied. Orders destined for Alaska and Hawaii incur a mandatory $12.00 surcharge across all available shipping tiers. International shipping starts at a base rate of $24.95 for the initial 2 lbs of parcel weight. During the checkout process, optional package protection insurance can be purchased for 1.5% of your total cart value to protect against transit loss. Please keep in mind that all specialized shipping surcharges and expedited postage fees are strictly non-refundable on returned orders.

## What should I do if my package is lost, stolen, or damaged?

Carriers officially mark packages as lost after 10 consecutive business days pass without any registered scanning activity. If your delivery is marked confirmed but your package was stolen, filing a reimbursement or replacement claim for values over $100 requires an official police report. Damage claims must be filed through support within 48 hours of carrier delivery along with supporting photographic evidence of the damage. If you purchased package protection insurance, it covers 100% of verified transit loss; without insurance, coverage is limited to a maximum of $100. Once your claim is approved, a replacement order is dispatched at no cost within 3 business days.
