# Northvale Chargeback and Payment Dispute Policy
## What is the difference between a Northvale billing dispute and a bank chargeback?

A Northvale billing dispute is an internal support ticket managed directly by customer service, whereas a bank chargeback is a formal dispute initiated through your card-issuing financial institution. Customers are strongly encouraged to utilize the internal dispute process first because it provides a significantly faster resolution than routing through external banking channels. Opening an internal ticket allows the support team to investigate transaction records immediately without requiring third-party banking intervention. The internal investigation assesses order logs, payment histories, and fulfillment details to determine eligibility for a direct store refund. External bank chargebacks involve complex inter-bank messaging systems that extend processing timelines considerably across multiple financial institutions.

## Within what timeframe must a payment dispute be filed?

A payment dispute must be filed within the mandatory 60-day dispute filing window starting from the original transaction charge date. Customers who fail to submit their formal dispute within this specified timeframe will find the transaction permanently ineligible for review. Northvale systems automatically enforce this cutoff to maintain compliance with standard payment processing regulations and merchant guidelines. Submitting documentation early ensures that support agents can access recent order logs and carrier delivery telemetry without archival delays. Please review your transaction history regularly to identify any discrepancies well before the expiration of the filing limit.

## How quickly does Northvale acknowledge and review a dispute ticket?

Northvale acknowledges any newly submitted dispute ticket within an automated acknowledgment SLA of 24 hours from the time of initial submission. Following acknowledgment, the internal review process officially begins within 2 business days. A final resolution decision on the internal ticket is then reached and communicated within 10 business days. Every submitted ticket is assigned a unique tracking reference number that customers can use to monitor review progress. Support representatives utilize this reference to pull up all gathered evidence and notes during status inquiries.

## How fast is a refund issued if an internal dispute is approved?

An approved internal dispute triggers a refund execution within 5 business days for standard electronic processing. If electronic disbursement fails, a manual reissue process is initiated within 24 hours to secure the funds. All approved refunds are returned exclusively to the original payment method used during the initial purchase transaction. Customers should allow standard banking processing days for the credited funds to appear on their monthly card statements after execution. Northvale does not issue cash or alternative payment credits when resolving standard internal transaction disputes.

## When is a police report mandatory for an unauthorized charge claim?

A formal police report becomes strictly mandatory for any unauthorized charge claim where the disputed transaction amount exceeds $500. Customers submitting claims above this dollar amount must provide the official police report number directly to the billing team during ticket creation. Failure to supply a valid police report for high-value fraudulent charge claims will result in immediate suspension of the internal dispute review. The billing team verifies the submitted report details with local law enforcement jurisdictions to combat fraudulent account activity. Transactions valued below this specific monetary threshold do not require official police documentation for standard internal processing.

## What happens to my account while a bank chargeback is open?

Accounts subject to an active bank chargeback experience a temporary account ordering restriction while the financial institution investigates the transaction. This ordering restriction prevents any new purchases from being charged to the locked account until the external dispute is fully resolved. Furthermore, any concurrent internal refunds associated with the account are automatically paused while the bank dispute remains open. Northvale implements these temporary security measures to protect both parties from duplicate credit issuance during active banking litigation. Once the card issuer closes the chargeback case, the account restrictions are reviewed for potential removal.

## What is the card issuer response window for chargeback representment?

The card issuer response window for chargeback representment spans the mandatory range of 20 to 45 days depending on the card network. During this specific timeframe, Northvale compiles comprehensive merchant evidence to contest the cardholder's dispute through acquiring bank channels. The merchant evidence package is transmitted back to the issuing bank before the expiration of the established window to defend the transaction validity. Issuers review the submitted representment data against cardholder claims to render a final verdict on the chargeback. Customers must await the conclusion of this formal review period for the issuing bank to communicate its binding decision.

## What compelling evidence does Northvale submit in chargeback representment?

Northvale submits comprehensive compelling evidence during chargeback representment, including detailed order logs, AVS and 3DS match verification records, and IP device telemetry. In addition to digital verification data, the representment package includes official carrier delivery proof and signature-verified delivery records confirming package handover. This robust collection of electronic and physical documentation proves that the transaction was authorized and fulfilled correctly. Acquiring banks evaluate these records to determine whether the cardholder's claim of unauthorized use or non-delivery is valid. The compiled telemetry establishes clear digital and physical links between the cardholder and the fulfilled purchase.

## How does signed delivery proof affect "item not received" chargebacks?

Signed delivery proof and carrier GPS confirmation carry decisive weight in defeating item not received chargebacks during the representment process. When a recipient signature or verified delivery scan is present, card issuers generally rule in favor of the merchant regarding physical fulfillment. This signed verification distinguishes confirmed carrier delivery from porch theft or misplaced packages occurring after drop-off. Northvale submits these signed delivery records to demonstrate that the merchandise successfully reached the designated shipping address. Consequently, claims asserting non-delivery are routinely denied by card issuers when valid signature records are provided.

## What happens if I win or lose a bank chargeback?

Winning a bank chargeback results in the customer's provisional credit becoming permanent through the card-issuing financial institution. Conversely, if the chargeback is lost or ruled in favor of the merchant, Northvale executes an account balance reconciliation to recover the disputed funds. This reconciliation may involve reversing any provisional credits and applying the chargeback amount back to the customer account balance. Customers who lose a chargeback are responsible for paying the outstanding balance to restore full account standing and ordering privileges. All final outcomes are dictated by the binding rulings issued by the card network and the issuing bank.

## How do I withdraw a chargeback after resolving the issue with Northvale?

Withdrawing a chargeback after resolving an issue directly with Northvale requires contacting your card issuer immediately to formally cancel the active dispute. Customers must request a written dispute withdrawal letter or confirmation notice from their bank and forward it to support staff. Upon receiving the confirmed bank withdrawal documentation, Northvale lifts any temporary account locks within standard processing timeframes. Direct communication with the issuing bank is essential because merchants lack the technical capability to withdraw external bank disputes on behalf of cardholders. Resolving the matter internally first prevents unnecessary chargeback fees and eliminates the need for lengthy banking representment cycles.

## Can a closed internal dispute be reopened later?

A closed internal dispute can be reopened only if the request occurs within the strict 30-day reopen window following the initial closure date. Requests submitted after this specified window have elapsed will not be considered, and customers must instead file a completely fresh dispute. Furthermore, reopening a closed ticket requires submitting brand new supporting evidence that was not included in the original review. Support specialists evaluate the newly provided documentation to determine if a reversal of the previous closure decision is warranted. Cases lacking sufficient new supporting evidence will remain permanently closed in accordance with store policy.

## How are duplicate-charge disputes handled internally vs via chargeback?

Duplicate-charge disputes handled internally undergo fast verification and immediate refund execution by support agents upon reviewing transaction logs. When customers report double-billing through the internal support channel, agents cross-reference payment gateway logs to confirm the accidental duplicate payment. In contrast, filing a duplicate-charge dispute via a bank chargeback takes significantly longer due to multi-party financial investigation workflows. Internal processing bypasses the lengthy card network arbitration steps, allowing funds to return to the customer much faster. Utilizing the internal route for duplicate charges is therefore the most efficient method for securing a prompt correction.

## What is "friendly fraud" and how does Northvale handle repeated chargebacks?

Friendly fraud occurs when a customer purchases and receives a valid item but wrongfully disputes the charge with their bank instead of seeking a refund. Northvale actively monitors transaction histories to identify patterns of abusive dispute behavior and repeated unfounded chargeback claims. Confirmed abuse of the dispute system results in permanent account suspension and the restriction of all future purchasing capabilities. The platform preserves detailed digital logs and delivery proofs to contest friendly fraud effectively during card issuer representment. Protecting the marketplace from fraudulent chargeback activity ensures fair pricing and reliable service for all genuine shoppers.

## Who do I contact for status updates on an open representment case?

Status updates for an open representment case should be requested by contacting the specialized billing and disputes support queue via the primary help portal. Support agents within this dedicated queue can provide tracking information regarding evidence compilation and representment submission timelines. However, customers must remember that the card issuer ultimately makes the final chargeback ruling on all external banking disputes. While Northvale support can share submission statuses, the final decision timeline rests entirely with the card-issuing financial institution. Regular communication with the billing team ensures customers remain informed throughout every stage of the active banking dispute.
