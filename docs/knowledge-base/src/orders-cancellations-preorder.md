# Northvale Pre-Order and Cancellation Policy
## When can I cancel an advance pre-order without penalty?

You can cancel an advance pre-order without penalty at any time prior to the pre-order cancellation deadline of 48 hours before the release date. Customers can perform this action directly through the self-service cancellation option located in their online order history. Northvale processes these timely requests immediately without charging any administrative fees. The system automatically releases the reserved item back into the general inventory pool upon confirmation. You will receive an email confirmation once the cancellation is successfully recorded.

## Why are pre-order cancellations blocked close to release?

Pre-order cancellations are blocked close to release because the order enters a mandatory pre-release cutoff window where item allocation locks. Northvale initiates warehouse staging and carrier manifest preparation during this final phase to ensure timely fulfillment. Once this automated locking process begins, the fulfillment system cannot intercept the package. Customers attempting to cancel after this window must wait until delivery and follow standard return procedures. Northvale enforces this restriction to maintain efficient logistics operations across all distribution centers.

## How do cancellations work for backordered items?

You are permitted to cancel backordered items anytime prior to warehouse allocation. Customers can navigate to their order history and select the specific backordered line item to cancel it independently. Northvale leaves all other non-backordered items in the active order unaffected by this partial cancellation. The system updates the remaining balance and sends an updated invoice summary to the customer. Once the backordered item reaches the warehouse allocation stage, this self-service cancellation option closes.

## What happens if a pre-order release date is delayed by the manufacturer?

Northvale grants a penalty-free cancellation right immediately whenever a manufacturer slips the scheduled release date. Customers receive a proactive email notice detailing the revised ship dates as soon as the manufacturer confirms the delay. You maintain the right to cancel your order at any point during this waiting period without incurring penalties. Northvale automatically updates the expected delivery timeline across all customer account dashboards. If you choose not to wait for the delayed release, you can submit a cancellation request through your account interface.

## How are bulk or custom-manufactured orders cancelled?

Bulk and custom-manufactured orders require a mandatory cancellation notice of 24 hours prior to production commencement. If you cancel a custom or bulk order after production starts, Northvale applies a cancellation fee of 15% to cover incurred manufacturing costs. Customers must submit their cancellation requests through customer support to coordinate special manufacturing halts. Northvale reviews each custom request against the production schedule to determine eligibility. Approved cancellations reflect the deduction of the aforementioned fee before any remaining funds are returned.

## Can I cancel one item from a split-shipment order?

You can cancel any unshipped item from a split-shipment order within the standard cancellation cutoff window. Customers may isolate individual line items in their order management dashboard and submit a cancellation for those specific unfulfilled goods. Any items within the split shipment that have already shipped must follow the standard return window after delivery. Northvale calculates adjusted totals for the remaining active items automatically. You will see the updated shipment details reflected in your final confirmation email.

## How long does warehouse processing take before an order ships?

Warehouse processing takes a standard processing-to-shipping window of 24 to 48 hours before an order officially ships. Northvale explicitly blocks cancellations once the carrier pickup scans the package into the transit network. Customers must submit any cancellation requests well in advance of this logistics milestone to ensure successful interception. Once the carrier scan is recorded, the fulfillment phase is legally and operationally complete. You must subsequently rely on standard post-delivery return methods if the cancellation window has expired.

## What happens to orders that remain unfulfilled due to inventory shortage?

Orders that remain unfulfilled due to persistent inventory shortages undergo automatic unfulfilled-order expiration accompanied by a prompt customer notification. Northvale initiates a full refund on all automatically cancelled orders immediately following the expiration event. Customers do not need to take any manual action to trigger this automatic safety protocol. The system updates the order status to expired and sends a formal closure notice via email. Northvale ensures that no charges remain active for inventory that cannot be successfully allocated.

## When is a pre-order payment captured vs authorized?

Northvale places an initial authorization hold at checkout, but actual payment capture occurs strictly at the time of dispatch. Customers will only see a pending authorization on their financial statements while the pre-order remains unfulfilled. If you cancel a pre-order before dispatch, Northvale initiates the release of authorization holds according to standard banking protocols. The captured payment rule only activates once the item leaves the warehouse facility. This distinction ensures customers are never permanently billed for merchandise prior to physical shipment.

## How quickly is a refund initiated after a pre-order cancellation?

Northvale enforces an automated refund initiation SLA of 2 hours after a pre-order cancellation is confirmed. Customers receive an official email confirmation receipt immediately upon the successful initiation of the refund transaction. This automated workflow eliminates manual delays in transmitting refund instructions to the payment processor. Northvale logs the exact timestamp of initiation for transparent customer tracking. Financial institutions then process the transferred funds according to their respective institution guidelines.

## How long do cancelled pre-order refunds take to appear on cards?

Cancelled pre-order refunds take a standard refund processing timeline of 5 to 7 business days for major credit cards, and up to 10 business days for debit cards. Northvale releases the funds immediately, but banking networks require this additional duration for proper settlement and posting. Customers should differentiate between the initial drop-off of the pending authorization and the final posting of the credit on their statement. Financial institutions apply these processing days uniformly across standard and debit card transactions. You can contact your card issuer directly if funds do not appear after the stated maximum business days.

## Can I cancel a standard in-stock order while it is processing?

You can cancel a standard in-stock order while it is processing provided you act within the standard processing cancellation cutoff window of 24 hours. Northvale allows self-service cancellations inside this designated timeframe via the online order management dashboard. If the 24-hour cutoff has already passed, the system disables the cancellation option due to active warehouse fulfillment. Customers must then use the standard return process once the package arrives at the destination. Northvale provides tracking information to help customers monitor their shipment status in real time.

## Are promotional discounts preserved if I cancel one item in a pre-order?

Promotional discounts are subject to bundle and tier threshold recalculation whenever you cancel one item in a multi-item pre-order. Northvale automatically evaluates whether the remaining items still meet the original promotion criteria. Customers are shown a clear total preview before confirming the cancellation in their account portal. If the removal of an item breaches a discount threshold, the system adjusts the final price of the retained goods accordingly. Northvale requires explicit customer agreement to the recalculated total before finalizing the partial cancellation.

## What if I miss the pre-order cancellation cutoff?

Customers who miss the pre-order cancellation cutoff must utilize the post-delivery return option within the standard return window. Northvale requires that you accept the delivery and subsequently initiate a standard return request through the online portal. Standard return shipping rules and associated guidelines apply to all packages processed in this manner. Northvale customer support cannot override the pre-order cancellation cutoff once warehouse staging is finalized. You will be responsible for following the standard return instructions once the carrier completes the delivery.

## Can I reinstate a pre-order after cancelling it?

Cancellations are strictly final and immediately release the previously allocated inventory back into the public catalog. Northvale does not possess a mechanism to reinstate a pre-order once the cancellation transaction is fully processed. Customers wishing to acquire the item must instead place a new pre-order subject to current inventory availability and pricing. Northvale cannot guarantee that identical pre-order bonuses or queue positions will remain available upon re-ordering. You are encouraged to review your order details thoroughly before confirming any cancellation action.
