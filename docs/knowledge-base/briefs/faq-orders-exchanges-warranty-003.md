## Subtopic intent
Post-30-day manufacturer defect replacements, out-of-stock exchange fallbacks, and serial-number verification requirements. This document answers customer and support questions specific to warranty within orders/exchanges. Does not cover instant size/color swaps, temporary card holds, or change-of-mind return shipping fees.
